單句模式

I'm outside my hotel, heading to the city museum. My eSIM isn't working, so I ask the driver. Hi, does this bus go towards the city museum? Yeah, this one's going towards the museum and Riverside Park. Great, which stop's near the main door? You'll want Museum Square, across from the entrance. Can you let me know when we get there? Sure, sit near the front so you can hear me. Thanks, roaming's off, so I can't check the map. No problem, I'll give you a shout.

I get my card out before stepping onto the bus. It says contactless only, so I can't use cash, right? That's right, just tap your card on the reader here. I've got my travel card ready. Perfect, hold it there till the light turns green. It's green now, so I've paid. Do I need to tap again when I get off? No, just tap once when you get on this bus. Okay, I'll put my card away. That's fine, and keep your bag out of the way.

Near the museum, the driver tells me about a change. Museum Square's closed today, but you can get off at Library Road instead. Sorry, which stop did you say I should use? Library Road, one stop before Museum Square. Library Road, got it, and how far's the walk? About five minutes, and you'll see signs for the museum. Can you let me know when we reach Library Road? Of course, I'll tell you before we get there. Should I press the bell when you tell me? Yes, press it once, then get off through the back. Thanks, I'll get off there and follow the signs. I've got the right bus and a clear backup plan.
