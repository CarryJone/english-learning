Finding the Hotel After a Station Exit Change
播放速度
🎯 Mission
車站出口改變後，從中央車站走到飯店
你抵達 Central Station，原本要走三號出口，但告示說今天關閉。你要讀懂告示與地圖，問清楚五號出口，再依藍色標誌、書店與紅綠燈找到飯店側門。
成功條件：找出 Exit Three 關閉、改用 Exit Five、飯店步行八分鐘與 side entrance；說出 “Which exit should I use?”、 “Could you show me the way?”，並能複述左轉和過馬路的路線。
英文輸入：station notice + map result + information-desk conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行開口公共英文英文資訊判讀日常反應
今天的能力證據：從 station notice 找出 Exit Three 關閉與 Exit Five 替代出口，再從地圖抓到八分鐘步行、左轉、過馬路與 side entrance；用兩句目標語塊向工作人員確認路線。

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I get off at Central Station with my suitcase. My hotel is near the old town. I open the map on my phone. The map shows a short walk there. A station notice says, Exit Three is closed today. Please use Exit Five and follow the blue signs. The map says the hotel is eight minutes on foot. I save the hotel address before I leave. I see an information desk near the gates. I want to check the walking route. I do not want to miss the hotel entrance.

I ask a staff member, Which exit should I use? The staff member points to Exit Five. She says, Follow the blue signs to the street. I ask, Could you show me the way? She opens the map and points left. She says, Turn left after the bookstore. Then cross the street at the traffic light. It takes eight minutes to walk there. I repeat the directions in my own words. Exit Three is closed, but Exit Five is open. I thank her and leave the station.

I follow the blue signs and pass a small cafe. The street is busy, so I wait. I cross the street after the light turns green. I see a fountain near the old town gate. The map says the hotel is behind the fountain. I check the address one more time. A second notice says, Hotel guests use the side entrance. I walk around the building and find the side door. The door has my hotel name. I ask a guest, Is this the hotel entrance? The guest says, Yes, the front desk is upstairs. Now I can follow directions after an exit change.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 今天最值得主動說的是 Which exit should I use? 和 Could you show me the way?；前者確認出口，後者請對方指路。
  • 看到車站告示時，先抓 closed、exit、side entrance；再用 follow the blue signs 把資訊變成下一步。
  • 過馬路前先找 traffic light；把 cross the street 和 on foot 一起練，旅行時就能說清楚路線。
