🎯 Mission
拖著行李找到通往機場列車的電梯
你拿著機場列車的票，想推著重行李上樓。先看車站告示，再問站務員怎麼走。
✅ 從告示找出機場列車在哪一層，以及哪個設備目前不能用。
✅ 問清楚電梯在哪裡、要按哪一層。
✅ 出電梯後找到閘門，刷票上車。
英文輸入為模擬車站告示
AIRPORT TRAINS
Upper level · Level 2
Have your ticket ready at the gate.
Escalator out of order.

開口確認。Can I take the lift to the station?

先做 10 分鐘
  1. 讀 3 分鐘。先讀上方車站告示的四行英文，用中文回答：列車在第幾層？哪個設備不能用？
    核對答案第 2 層。手扶梯不能用。卡住時，把 Upper level · Level 2 和 Escalator · out of order 各自分開讀。
  2. 聽 4 分鐘。先播放一句，聽聽電梯在哪裡。答過再打開文字。
    核對原句If you've got heavy bags, / there's a lift just inside the car park.
    電梯就在停車場入口內側。先分兩段讀，再聽一次，確認自己漏聽哪一小段。
  3. 說 3 分鐘。在車站問「我可以搭電梯上去嗎？」先自己說，再核對。明天換到旅館，試著問能否搭電梯去房間。
    核對參考句Can I take the lift upstairs?

That's it. Keep your ticket out till you're through. Great, I'll take the escalator and go up that way.

At the bottom, I see a sign across the escalator. It's out of order. Is there another way up? Sure, we've got a lift as well. Oh, good. I don't see one from here. If you've got heavy bags, there's a lift just inside the car park. Sorry, where's the lift again? Just inside the car park. How do I get to the car park from here? Go through that short tunnel and turn right. Thanks. I'll take the lift from there.

I look through the tunnel from where we're standing. Oh, I can see the sign for the lift now. Yep, that's it. Press two for the station. Does it open right by the ticket gate? Yes, it opens on the same level as the gate. The doors on the other side will open upstairs. Good, I can roll my bag straight out that way. Which way do I turn when I get out? The gate's on your left, just past the map. Thanks, that'll save me from carrying this bag upstairs. I roll my bag through the gate and catch my train.

out of orderFixed Expression今日目標
故障；暫停使用

🎭 Role-play

換到博物館。遮住「你」的英文，先問能否搭電梯上樓，再問清楚哪一座故障、要去哪裡搭另一座。

你
Can I take the lift to the museum shop?
館員
One lift's out of order today, but you can still get upstairs.
你
Sorry, which lift is out of order?
館員
The one by the entrance. Try the lift near the café.
你
Where's the café from here?
館員
Go through those doors. It's on your left.
你
Thanks. I'll take the lift near the café.
館員
You're welcome. The shop's on level three.
