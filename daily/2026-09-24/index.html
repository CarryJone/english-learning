🎯 Mission
出發前在旅行用品店挑對英國轉接器
讀懂店內商品牌，選出適用於英國、今天有現貨的轉接器；確認折扣後金額和換貨條件，再向店員確認自己的充電器能否搭配使用。
完成條件：指出基礎款適用於英國且今天有現貨；確認 $12 減 $2 後為 $10，以及未使用、附收據可在 14 天內換貨；檢查充電器標示為 100–240 V，並說出「Will this work with my phone charger?」

單句模式

I'm near my hotel in a travel shop. Hi, can I help you find something? I'm leaving for the UK tomorrow, and I need an adapter. Can you help me pick one? Sure, what kind of phone charger have you got? It says 100 to 240 volts on the label. Great, your charger's fine for the UK. So I only need an adapter for the plug shape? Right, it changes the plug shape, not the voltage.

I compare two adapters on the shelf. This basic one fits UK outlets only, right? Yes, and it's twelve dollars. Does the two-dollar discount still apply today? Yep, that brings the amount down to ten dollars. Nice, that's a good price. Is the basic one in stock now? The other one's ready tomorrow, but we've got the UK one right here today. Sorry, which one's ready today? The UK one's here today. Great, I'll take that one. Do you want to check the exchange rule? Yes, how many days have I got? You've got fourteen days to bring it back unused. Do I need to keep the receipt? Yeah, bring it back with the receipt.

I read one more line on the product card. It says the adapter changes the plug shape only. Exactly, the voltage stays the same. Can I plug my charger into this adapter? Sure, let's try it with this display model. Will this work with my phone charger? Yep, it'll work with your charger. Perfect, I'll pack it for my trip. Now I know what to buy and what to check.
