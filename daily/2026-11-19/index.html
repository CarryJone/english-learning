單句模式

I'm cycling to an island to see my cousin's new café. At the ferry dock, I see stairs beside the ticket counter. Hi, can I take my bike on the ferry? Sure, there's room for bikes on the next one. Great, I'd like a ticket to the island, please. That's six pounds, and the bike goes free. Thanks, I'll pay by card. Here you go. Just wait behind that yellow line. I've got a sandwich in this bag on the bike. Should I take the bag off before getting on? You can leave it there if it's fixed on properly.

I check the bag, then look back at the stairs. Will I have to carry the bike down those stairs? No, those lead to the small boats. That's a relief. This bike's quite heavy. When the ferry comes in, we'll put the ramp down. So I can just push the bike onto the ferry? Exactly. Walk beside it, and we'll show you where to park. The ferry pulls in, and a few people get off. You can get on once the last passenger's off, but wait behind the line. Sorry, should I go now or wait here? Wait here till everyone's off. Got it, I'll stay behind the line. Okay, you're good to go now.

I push my bike up the ramp beside the same worker. Put it against that rail. I'll tie it on for you. Thanks. Can I sit upstairs during the trip? Sure, take anything you'll need out of your bag first. Good idea. I'll take my sandwich and leave the bike here. From upstairs, I spot my cousin's café across the water. She's outside waving, so I wave back with my sandwich.

Can I take my bike on the train?

Yes. Your bike goes free after nine, but before nine there's a charge.
你
It's ten now, so the bike goes free, right?
站務員
That's right. Use the lift to platform two. The stairs are closed.
你
Sorry, which platform did you say?
站務員
Platform two.
你
Thanks. I'll take the lift.
