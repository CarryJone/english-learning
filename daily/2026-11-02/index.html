🎯 Mission
渡輪取消後，確認下一班與正確登船位置
你在國外碼頭準備去島上，原班次取消了。讀公告，再向同一位票務人員確認原票、時間與上船位置。
✅ 找出取消班次、下一班時間與碼頭編號。
✅ 確認原票可搭下一班，不必再買票。
✅ 聽懂室內候船到 10:20，之後到 Gate B 排隊。
英文輸入：渡輪服務公告（教學用模擬，不代表真實航線規則）
ISLAND FERRY — SERVICE UPDATE
10:00 sailing: cancelled.
Next sailing: 10:30, Pier 3.
Tickets for 10:00: valid on the next sailing.
Crossing time: 25 minutes.
Upper deck: closed today.
Boarding: Gate B. Please follow staff instructions.

開口完成任務：Can I use this ticket for the next sailing?

單句模式

I'm at the ferry terminal with my ticket ready. My trip to the island starts with a change. Excuse me, is the ten o'clock ferry still running? Sorry, that sailing's cancelled, but there's another one soon. Oh, can I use this ticket for the next sailing? Sure, you don't need to buy a new ticket. That's good, because I've already paid for this one. The next sailing's at ten thirty from Pier Three, right? That's right, and there's still space for you today. Great, I'd like to get on that ferry, please.

I stay at the desk to check the crossing. How long's the crossing from here to the island? It's about twenty-five minutes, so it won't take long. Can I sit on the upper deck for the view? Sorry, the upper deck's closed, but we've got seats inside. That's fine, I'd rather stay warm on the crossing. No problem, you can take your small bag inside. Should I keep my paper ticket in my hand? Yes, please have it ready when you get on. Okay, I'll keep it somewhere safe until you need it.

Before I walk away, the same staff member adds something. You'll wait inside till ten twenty, then head out to Gate B. Sorry, when should I head out to the gate? Ten twenty, Gate B. So I'll wait inside first, then go to Gate B. Exactly, it's just through those doors on your left. Will someone check my ticket before I get on? Yes, I'll check it at the gate before boarding. Thanks, I'll follow the signs and keep my ticket ready. You're welcome, and I hope you enjoy the island. I've got my next ferry sorted without buying another ticket.

💡 Learning Tips
主動背 Can I use this ticket for the next sailing?；看公告先找 next sailing 與碼頭，sailing、crossing 先以辨識交通資訊為主。
聽到時間藏在長句裡，先問 When should I head out?，確認 10:20 到 Gate B；不要把開船的 10:30 當成去排隊的時間。
