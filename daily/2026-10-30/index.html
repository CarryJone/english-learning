搭晚班火車前想寄放行李。你有一個高 65 公分、寬 40 公分的行李箱，先讀網站，再用客服對話確認可用方案。
✅ 比較行李箱與小置物櫃的尺寸上限，知道放不下。
✅ 改選有人服務的 luggage desk，找到地面層入口旁。
✅ 分清小置物櫃與寄放櫃檯的關門時間，安排六點領回並帶行李牌。

單句模式

I'm looking online for luggage storage before my train. I've got a suitcase, so I open the help chat. Hi, can I leave my suitcase here for the afternoon? Sure, let's check which option works for your bag. I'm looking at the small lockers online. Okay, check the maximum bag size first. It says the maximum height is forty centimeters, right? That's right, and the maximum width is thirty centimeters. Mine's sixty-five centimeters tall, so it won't fit. We've got a luggage desk for larger bags.

I compare the options before choosing where to go. Can I use the luggage desk instead? Of course, someone there will tag your bag. Is the desk in the same building as the lockers? Yes, it's on the ground floor, beside the main entrance. Great, can I download my booking after I pay? Sure, download it and keep it on your phone. Can I show it offline when I drop off my bag? Yes, make sure the booking number is clear. What time do I need to pick it up? The lockers stay open till nine, but the desk shuts at six thirty. Sorry, what time does the luggage desk close? Six thirty, so come back before then.

I check the pickup time against my travel plans. I'll pick it up at six, before my train leaves. That works, bring the bag tag back with you. Can I check the width of my suitcase there too? Sure, but your bag's too tall for our small lockers. Right, I'll use the luggage desk and keep my tag safe. Great, choose the luggage desk option when you book online. Thanks, I'll save my booking before heading out. I've checked the size limit, location, and latest pickup time.

🔗 Key Phrases
maximum bag sizeCollocation今日目標
行李尺寸上限
今日目標：讀懂 maximum bag size，先比高度與寬度再決定方案。
pick it upPhrasal Verb今日目標
領取它
今日目標：I’ll pick it up at six；確認領取時間，不能套用另一方案的營業時間。
insteadFixed Expression今日目標
改為；代替原選項
今日目標：Can I ... instead? 原方案不適用時改問替代辦法。
drop offPhrasal Verb
寄放；送到
drop off my bag 是送行李來，pick it up 是回來領。
ground floorCollocation
地面層；一樓
本篇寄放櫃檯的位置；不同國家的樓層編號要再確認。
head outPhrasal Verb
出發；出門
出發前先存好訂單，避免到現場找不到。

💡 Learning Tips
  • 看網頁先圈出 maximum bag size、height、width，尺寸不合就問 Can I leave it at the luggage desk instead?；maximum 先以認得資訊上限為目標。
  • 不要把置物櫃的九點關門套到有人服務的櫃檯；先問清楚，再說 I’ll pick it up at six.，並帶行李牌。drop off 是送來寄放，pick up 是領回。
