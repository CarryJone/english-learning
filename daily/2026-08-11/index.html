Taking a Harbor Ferry After a Pier Change
播放速度
🎯 Mission
看懂渡輪資訊，並在碼頭變更後找到正確位置
你要從港口看板找出出發時間、碼頭、票價與回程時間；買票後遇到碼頭臨時更改，要向工作人員再次確認並請他在地圖上指出位置。
成功條件：找出 10:20、Pier Three、12 美元與最後回程 5:00；說出 “Which pier does it leave from?”；改到 Pier Five 並理解延誤 10 分鐘。
英文輸入：ferry board + change notice + short ticket-window conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
公共英文旅行開口日常反應英文資訊判讀
今天的能力證據：從渡輪看板找出 10:20、Pier Three、$12 round-trip ticket 與 5:00 return ferry；碼頭改到 Pier Five 時，用短問句再次確認並請工作人員在地圖上指出位置。

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I want to visit Island Park by ferry. I reach the harbor before ten o'clock. A large board shows today's ferry times. It says, “Island Park Ferry, 10:20.” The board also shows Pier Three. A round-trip ticket costs twelve dollars. The last return ferry leaves at five. I write these three details in my phone. Then I walk to the ticket window.

I'd like one round-trip ticket, please. Certainly. Will you pay by card? Yes. Which pier does it leave from? It leaves from Pier Three today. Great. Could you show me on the map? Of course. Walk past that bench. Pier Three is beside the small cafe.

I follow the map toward the water. Small boats fill the busy harbor. I find Pier Three and check the sign. It says, “Island Park, boarding at 10:10.” I keep my ticket ready in my hand. Suddenly, a message appears on the board.

“Pier change: Island Park Ferry, Pier Five.” “Departure delayed ten minutes.” I hear a fast announcement behind me. I understand “Island Park,” but miss the number. I ask a worker near the gate. Sorry, which pier does it leave from now? Pier Five. The ferry is ten minutes late. Could you show me on the map? Sure. Go back past the ticket window. Then turn left after the long bench.

I reach Pier Five with time to spare. I show my ticket at the gate. I get on and choose the upper deck. I will return before the five o'clock ferry.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 今天先抓三個重複聲音：Pier Three、Pier Five、ten minutes。第一次聽時只找這些資訊。
  • 最值得主動說的是 Which pier does it leave from?；位置還是不清楚，就接 Could you show me on the map?。
  • round-trip ticket 是買交通票時的實用搭配；看見 departure delayed，先找延誤多久與新的出發位置。
