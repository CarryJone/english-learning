🎯 Mission
伴手禮有瑕疵：說明問題、確認缺貨與退款方式
昨天買的藍杯有裂痕和缺角，還沒有使用。帶著收據回店裡，先問更換；原色缺貨時改選退款。
✅ 說出裂痕與缺角的位置，確認商品未使用並出示收據。
✅ 看懂藍色售完，聽清楚可換綠色或退款。
✅ 確認 £8 退回原卡，並保存退款收據。
英文輸入：商店退換貨標示（教學用模擬，非真實商店政策）
RETURNS DESK
Unused items: bring the item and your receipt for a check.
Blue mugs: SOLD OUT
Green mugs: available
Damaged items: ask staff about a replacement or refund.

單句模式

I'm back at the shop with yesterday's blue mug. I show the shop assistant my mug. Hi, this mug has a crack. Sorry about that, can you show me where? It's near the handle, and the top's chipped too. I can see it, thanks for letting us know. It's a gift, so I haven't used it at all. Have you got your receipt with you? Yes, I'm here to bring it back with the receipt. Thanks, I'll check it for you.

I read the returns sign while waiting. It says unused items need a receipt, and mine's unused. That's right, and you've brought everything we need. Great, I'd like another blue mug without any damage. Let me check what's on the shelf. They're fragile, so can I check the next one first? Of course, take your time and have a good look. Thanks, I don't want to give a broken gift. I understand, we'll sort this out for you. If there's no blue one, can I get a refund? I'll check, then tell you the options.

The assistant comes back with news about the blue mugs. Blue's sold out, but we've got green or a refund to your card. Sorry, what can I get instead of blue? A green mug, or a refund to your card. I see, the sign says blue mugs are sold out. Yes, I'm afraid we haven't got any blue ones left. I'll get a refund, then, back to my card, please. Sure, that's eight pounds, and I'll give you a refund receipt. Thanks, so the full eight pounds goes back to my card? Exactly, keep this receipt until you see the money come back. I've explained the damage and checked the refund amount and method.

💡 Learning Tips
