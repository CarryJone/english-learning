單句模式

I've got an hour before lunch with my friend. I'd love to make her a card with her favourite bird. At the museum desk, I show the worker a workshop photo. Hi, could I make a card like this? Oh, that's from our bird print workshop. I saw an eleven o'clock class on your website. That one's full, but we've got space at one. I'll be at lunch then, so I can't stay. We've got a drop-in table if you've only got a little time. Oh, I skipped that part of the page. It's under Try It Yourself.

Do I need to book for the drop-in table? No, you can start right now. It says materials included. Does that cover an envelope too? Yep, the paper, ink and envelopes are all on the table. Great, I'll give it a try.

I pay, and the worker shows me to the table. There's the stamp with the blue bird. Press it onto the paper like this. Oh, look at that little line under its feet. That's a branch. You can add leaves with this stamp. I'll just add one leaf so there's room to write. Leave it on this rack while the ink dries. I've got to leave before noon. It'll be dry in ten minutes if you leave it flat on the rack. Sorry, how long should I leave it there? Ten minutes on the rack. Perfect, I've still got twenty minutes. While it dries, I write her name on the envelope. It's dry now, so I can put it in here. At lunch, I hand my friend the card. She props it up by her plate.

🎭 Role-play

換到旅遊途中的烘焙活動。遮住「你」的英文，練習詢問預約、材料費及要帶的東西。

Are materials included in the fee?

Yes, but you'll need to bring a box for your cookies.
你
Sorry, what do I need to bring?
店員
A box for your cookies.
你
I've got a box in my bag. I'll give it a try.
