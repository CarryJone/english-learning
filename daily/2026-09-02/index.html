🎯 Mission
讀懂藥局公告，安全買到感冒藥
你要從藥局網頁確認營業時間、成人感冒藥是否供應，以及使用標示；到店後描述症狀，若第一個選項今天沒有，再詢問替代方案。
成功條件：找出開門與關門時間、確認藥品可否取得與注意事項，並能描述咳嗽、詢問替代品。
英文輸入：藥局網頁公告 + 藥師對話

單句模式

I wake with a sore throat on my second travel day. Before my tour, I look for a nearby pharmacy. The map shows Green Cross Pharmacy beside my hotel. It opens at eight and closes at ten tonight. I open the pharmacy page and read its health notice. The page says common cold medicine is available for adults. Please ask about your symptoms before choosing. Follow the label and do not take more. Some products may make you sleepy. Ask before driving or joining a long tour.

I show the page on my phone. Could you help me find a safe cold medicine? Please tell me about your symptoms. I have a cough, but no fever. This tablet medicine is available without a prescription. Please take one tablet after food and follow the label. Is it safe to take before my walking tour? This one may make you sleepy during the day. Then could you show me another option? The non-drowsy tablets are not available today. I want to understand before I decide.

The liquid medicine is available, but please read its label. Could you say that again more slowly, please? Read the label, and stop if you feel worse. I understand and will follow the label carefully. If symptoms continue, please visit a local clinic. I will ask about my symptoms at the clinic. I check the opening time again before leaving. The page says the pharmacy is open until ten. I buy the medicine and save the instructions. Now I can join the tour without guessing. The mission is complete: find, ask, check, choose safely.

🔗 Key Phrases
look for Phrasal Verb
尋找
look for a nearby pharmacy；搜尋地點、店家或服務時可直接套用。
ask about your symptoms Collocation
詢問你的症狀
藥局公告提醒你，在選產品前先 ask about your symptoms。
follow the label Fixed Expression
依照標示
看藥品、清潔用品或其他產品說明時，表示照著標示做；不是只說 follow。
take one tablet Collocation
服用一錠
搭配藥品說明使用，先讀清楚數量與時機。
make you sleepy Collocation
讓你想睡
用來理解產品提醒，例如 This medicine may make you sleepy。
without a prescription Fixed Expression
不需處方箋
在藥局網頁或產品說明上看到時，表示不需要醫師處方。
show me another option Fixed Expression
給我看看另一個選項
第一個商品、票種或方案不適用時，Could you show me another option? 很實用。
say that again more slowly Fixed Expression
再慢一點說一次
聽不清楚工作人員說明時，禮貌地請對方重複。

💡 Learning Tips
  • 今天最值得直接背的是 Could you show me another option?；藥品、票種、餐點或房型不適用時都能用。
  • 看藥局頁面先抓開關店時間、available、without a prescription 與 make you sleepy；使用產品時要 follow the label。
  • 任務成功的標準是找出時間與注意事項、說明自己的症狀；若第一個選項改變，先問替代方案，聽不清楚就說 Could you say that again more slowly, please?。
