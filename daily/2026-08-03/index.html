Handling a Stuck Hotel Laundry Machine
🎯 Mission
讀懂旅館洗衣間公告並處理卡住的洗衣機
你在旅館使用洗衣間時，發現洗衣機的門卡住了。先讀公告，再向櫃台說明問題、確認另一台設備是否正常，並在需要時安排維修或尋找替代方案。
成功條件：找出 Laundry Room B 在二樓、開放時間為早上 7 點到晚上 10 點，以及門卡住時不要拉門；說明故障、確認維修約需 30 分鐘，並問出替代方案。
英文輸入：hotel laundry notice + front-desk dialogue
🗺️ Ability Focus
日常反應公共英文旅行開口英文資訊判讀
今天的能力證據：Mission: handle a stuck hotel laundry machine. Input: laundry notice and front-desk dialogue. Find Floor 2, the 7 a.m.–10 p.m. hours, the do-not-pull rule, and the thirty-minute repair time. Say “The washer is stuck.” / “Is it working?” / “Could you call for a repair?”

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

Mina looks at the hotel laundry notice. It says, “Laundry Room B is on the second floor.” The room is open from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. Use one coin for each machine. Do not use a machine that is out of order.

If a door is stuck, do not pull it. Please call the front desk and report the problem. Mina starts the washer, but the door is stuck. She says, “The washer is stuck. Could you help me?” The clerk asks, “Is it working?”

Mina is not sure, so she looks at another machine. The clerk says, “The dryer is working, but Washer 2 is full.” Mina asks, “Could you call for a repair?” The clerk says, “Yes. A repair person can come in thirty minutes.” Mina asks about another option because the machine is not available. She makes a careful choice and waits near reception.

on the second floor Fixed Expression

be stuck Collocation

文章用法：the door is stuck → 門卡住、不能動。今日修復目標：直接說明機器問題。
out of order Fixed Expression
故障；暫停使用
公告中的 out of order 表示設備故障，看到它就不要使用那台機器。
call the front desk Collocation
打電話給櫃台；聯絡櫃台
遇到設備問題時，call the front desk 尋求旅館協助。

詢問另一台機器能不能使用。今日資訊確認目標：Is it working?
call for a repair Collocation
要求安排維修
文章用法：Could you call for a repair? → 請櫃台安排維修。今日修復目標。
another option Collocation
另一個方案；替代選擇

💡 Learning Tips
  • 先從公告抓出 Floor 2、開放時間 7 a.m.–10 p.m.、以及故障時不要拉門；這些是今天的任務驗收資訊。

  • 詢問另一台設備時先說 Is it working?；如果答案是否定的，再問 Is there another option?。
