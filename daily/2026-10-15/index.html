🎯 Mission
借來的熨斗不能用：說明問題並安排免費更換
你準備出門吃飯，卻發現借來的熨斗不會變熱。讀旅館設備協助頁面，再打電話給櫃檯確認替代方案。
✅ 看懂故障設備可免費更換，不需要把故障熨斗帶下樓。
✅ 分清晚上 9 點可自行領取，10 點才可送到房間。
✅ 說出 The iron doesn't heat up，並選擇領取時間。
英文輸入：旅館設備協助頁（教學用模擬）
EQUIPMENT HELP
Faulty equipment: stop using it and contact reception.
Replacement at no extra cost.
Leave the faulty item in your room.
Pickup and room delivery times: check with reception.

單句模式

I'm at my hotel, ready to go out. My clothes are in a basket, but the iron's cold. Hi, I'm in room twelve, and I've got a problem. Sorry about that, what isn't working in your room? The iron I borrowed this afternoon doesn't heat up. Okay, thanks for telling me, is anything else wrong? The cord looks fine, but the iron stays cold. Sounds faulty, let's get you a working one instead. Thanks, I'll turn it off and stop using it. That's right, leave it there for now, and I'll check.

I check the hotel's help page while waiting. It says faulty equipment is replaced at no extra cost. Exactly, you won't need to pay for another iron. Great, can I pick up another one at reception? Let me see when we've got a spare one ready. I'd like to iron my shirt before dinner. Of course, I'll see what we can do for you. Thanks, can I leave the faulty one in my room? Yes, we'll take it away, so leave it there. Okay, and I'll keep my clothes in the basket meanwhile. No problem, I'll get the replacement ready soon.

The receptionist comes back with two options. The spare's ready at nine, but room delivery won't work till ten. Sorry, what time can I pick it up myself? Nine for pickup, ten for room delivery. I'll pick it up at nine, then, before dinner. Sure, come to reception and tell me your room number. And that's at no extra cost, right? That's right, we'll swap it for a working one. Thanks, I'll come down at nine to pick it up. You're welcome, and sorry again about the faulty iron. I've reported the problem and arranged a replacement.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 先說 The iron doesn’t heat up，比只說 broken 更能讓對方知道問題；at no extra cost 要看懂，也要開口確認。
  • 聽到兩個時間別急著選：先問 What time can I pick it up?，分清自行領取與送到房間，再說 I’ll pick it up at nine。
