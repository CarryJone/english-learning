🎧 2-Minute A2 Dual-Voice Practice
Reporting a Missing Suitcase at Baggage Claim
播放速度
🎯 Mission
看懂行李提領資訊，並回報未出現的行李箱
你要從機場螢幕與服務告示找出正確轉盤、特殊行李位置和櫃台時間；再向行李服務人員描述行李，確認隔日配送。
成功條件：找出 Flight 428 的六號轉盤、大型行李在八號轉盤、服務櫃台開到 11:30 p.m.；說出 “My suitcase is missing.”；確認明早免費送到市區旅館。
英文輸入：baggage claim screen + help-desk notice + airport service conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行開口公共英文英文資訊判讀日常反應
今天的能力證據：從螢幕找出 Flight 428、carousel six、oversized bags 與 11:30 p.m.；用 “My suitcase is missing.” 回報問題，再用 “Could you help me find it?” 請求協助。

重要片語
🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I arrive at the busy baggage claim after my evening flight. A large screen says, Flight four-two-eight: Carousel Six. I walk slowly to carousel six with other passengers. Most checked bags come out within ten minutes. My medium black suitcase does not appear. I carefully check the screen again. Oversized bags: use carousel eight. Airport help desk open until eleven-thirty p.m. My suitcase is a normal size, not oversized. I look around carousel six one more time. I can finally see a nearby baggage claim desk. I take my small baggage tag from my passport.

Excuse me, where is the baggage claim desk? It is beside carousel eight. Thank you. My suitcase is missing. Could you help me find it? Of course. Which flight were you on? I was on Flight four-two-eight from Taipei this evening. Did you check the oversized-bag area? Sorry, could you say that more slowly? Did you check carousel eight? Yes, but my suitcase was not there. Please describe your suitcase. It is black with a blue handle. It also has a red name tag. I will enter your claim number into the system.
Then a new message suddenly appears on the screen. Flight four-two-eight: some bags delayed. Delivery to city hotels is free. Your suitcase is delayed, not lost. We can deliver it to your city hotel tomorrow morning. Will it arrive before ten? Yes, the driver will call before delivery. Please keep this claim number. I take a quick photo and save the number. I leave with a clear next step.
