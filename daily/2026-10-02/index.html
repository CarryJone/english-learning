🎯 Mission
在旅行餐廳選好晚餐、確認附餐並請醬汁分開放
看懂菜單的份量與價格，問清附餐；遇到米飯售完時改選馬鈴薯並確認不加價。
完成條件：找出雞肉餐價格與附餐、飲料是否包含；說出醬汁要分開放；確認替代配菜不加價。開口句：Can I have the sauce on the side, please?

單句模式

I'm at a small restaurant after visiting the city museum. I open the menu and ask the server for help. Hi, is there space for one more person tonight? Sure, we've got a table right here by the window. Thanks, is the chicken portion enough for one person? Yeah, it's a good size and comes with rice. Does it come with a drink as well? No, drinks are extra, but we can bring you water. That sounds good, and what's the soup like? It's tomato soup, and it's a nice light starter.

I put the menu down and get ready to order. I'd like the chicken, but is the sauce spicy? A little, but we can put it on the side. Great, can I have the sauce on the side, please? Of course, we'll bring it in a separate bowl. Thanks, I'd like to try a little first. No problem, and would you like a starter too? Just the chicken for now, and some water, please. Sure, I'll bring your water over in a minute. One more thing, do you take contactless cards here? Yeah, you can tap your card when you're ready to pay.

Before the server leaves, there's a change to my order. We're out of rice, but potatoes are available at no extra cost. Sorry, what can I have instead of rice? Potatoes, at no extra cost. Okay, I'll have potatoes instead of rice, then. Great, chicken with potatoes, and the sauce on the side. That's right, with a separate bowl for the sauce. Perfect, I'll get that started for you now. Thanks, and can I keep the menu for later? Of course, just let me know if you need anything. I've checked my meal and asked for what I need.
