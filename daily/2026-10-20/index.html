單句模式

I'm choosing a gift for my sister. I find two mugs and ask for help. Hi, I'd like these two mugs, one for my sister. Lovely, are they both gifts, or is one for you? The blue one's a gift, and the green one's for me. Would you like us to wrap the blue one? Yes, can you wrap it up, please? Of course, we've got nice paper for gifts. Thanks, and can I check both mugs for damage first? Sure, take your time, I'll get the paper.

I check the mugs and read the packing sign. They're fragile, so please pack them separately for the journey. No problem, we'll put paper around each one. Great, I don't want them knocking against each other. That's right, separate packing helps keep them safe. Can I put them in my carry-on bag afterwards? Sure, just leave enough space so nothing presses against them. I'll keep them away from my water bottle too. Good idea, keep the bag with you. The sign says gift wrap is free, is that right? Yes, and we've got a box as well.

Before paying, I ask which packing options cost extra. Gift wrap's free, but the box is two pounds if you want it. Sorry, what's free, and what costs two pounds? The wrap's free; the box is two pounds. Just the free gift wrap, then, without the extra box. That's fine, and we'll still pack the mugs separately. Thanks, that's eight pounds each, so sixteen pounds altogether? Exactly, and there won't be an extra charge for wrapping. Great, please wrap it up and pack them separately. Of course, I'll have them ready for you in a minute. I've checked the gifts, packing, and final price.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 先背 Can you wrap it up, please?；看到 Gift wrap: FREE，只表示禮物包裝免費，不代表額外盒子也免費。
  • 兩件易碎品就說 Please pack them separately。聽不清楚費用時先問 What’s free, and what costs two pounds?，再確認總價。
