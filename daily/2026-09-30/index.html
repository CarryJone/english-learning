🎯 Mission
出發前讀懂英國旅遊 eSIM 設定指南，讓上網方案在正確時間啟用

完成條件：選出可支援 10 日旅程的方案並找出流量；說出如何連 Wi-Fi、掃描 QR 碼與保留方案；確認 Data Roaming 要在落地後才開啟；練會一句「Can I scan the QR code now?」
英文輸入：旅遊 eSIM 方案比較頁與快速設定指南（模擬情境資料）
UK TRAVEL eSIM · PLAN OPTIONS
3-day plan · 1 GB data
15-day plan · 5 GB data

QUICK SETUP
Before travel:
1. Connect your phone to Wi-Fi.
2. Open Settings > Mobile Data > Add eSIM.
3. Scan the QR code and name the plan.
4. Keep Data Roaming off until landing.

After landing:
5. Choose the travel eSIM for mobile data.
6. Turn Data Roaming on.

Keep the plan installed. Do not delete it.

單句模式

I'm getting my phone ready for a trip to London. I'll be there for ten days, so I need enough data. I compare the plan duration and data. The fifteen-day plan gives me five gigabytes. That'll cover your whole trip. Great, I bought the UK travel eSIM.

I open the setup guide while I'm on Wi-Fi. I want to set it up before I fly. Sure. Go to Settings and choose Add eSIM. Then I scan the QR code with my camera? That's right. You'll see the new plan on your phone. Should I rename it? You can call it “UK Trip” if you like. I can still use my main line for calls, right? Yes, keep your main line for calls and texts. I read the last note before I close the page. The guide says to keep Data Roaming off until I land.

The plan's ready now, but keep Data Roaming off till you land. Sorry, when should I turn it on? After you land. Got it. I'll turn it on after you land. Then choose the travel eSIM for mobile data. Does the travel plan replace my regular number? No, calls stay on your main line. Okay. I don't need to remove the plan later? No. Leave it there for the whole trip. I save the steps and pack my charger. I'll connect to Wi-Fi first, then install the eSIM. I can choose the travel plan when I arrive. And I'll keep roaming off until I land.
