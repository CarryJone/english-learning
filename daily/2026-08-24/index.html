Finding the Right Airport Train After a Platform Change
播放速度
🎯 Mission
確認機場快線的正確月台，並處理月台變更
你要讀車站發車看板、車站告示與工作人員說法，找出機場快線的目的地、出發時間與月台；當班次延誤並改到六號月台時，再開口確認下一步。
成功條件：找出 Airport Express、6:30、先到四號月台後改到六號月台與延誤 20 分鐘；說出 “Which platform does it leave from?”、 “When does it depart?”，並確認 day pass 可搭替代班次。
英文輸入：departure screen + station notice + staff conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行開口公共英文英文資訊判讀日常反應
今天的能力證據：從 departure screen 找出 Airport Express、6:30 與 Platform Four；用 “Which platform does it leave from?”、 “When does it depart?” 確認，再讀 notice 得知延誤 20 分鐘並改到 Platform Six。

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I check the airport train page before leaving my hotel. I arrive at the central station with my small bag. The departure screen shows Airport Express, Platform Four. The next train will depart at six thirty. The page says the ride takes forty minutes. A day pass is valid on this train. I check the map and find the blue line. The airport train has two stops before Terminal Two. I save the platform number on my phone. The station notice says, Check the screen before you board. It also says, Arrive ten minutes early.

I walk to Platform Four and see a staff member. Which platform does it leave from? Airport Express leaves from Platform Four. When does it depart? It departs at six thirty. I check the screen again. The train is on time, so I get on with my bag. I keep my ticket ready. I do not want to miss the train. A passenger points to a sign beside the door. It says, Do not enter after the bell.

A new notice says, Airport Express is delayed by twenty minutes. The screen changes to Platform Six. I leave Platform Four and follow the blue line. Is the train still going to the airport? Yes, but the service will depart from Platform Six. Could you say that again more slowly? The train is running late. Is my day pass still valid? Yes, it is valid for the replacement train. I check the screen and walk to Platform Six. I get on the train and keep the new notice. Now I can check the screen and ask about a platform change.

🔗 Key Phrases
check the screen Collocation
查看螢幕／顯示看板
check the screen before you board → 上車前查看顯示看板；這是看交通資訊時很常見的動作。
leave from Phrasal Verb
從……出發／離開
leave from Platform Four → 從四號月台出發；可替換成 leave from Gate Two。
which platform does it leave from? Fixed Expression
它從哪個月台出發？
直接用來確認車次的月台；也可以把 platform 換成 pier，問渡輪從哪個碼頭出發。
when does it depart? Fixed Expression
它幾點出發？
看到班次或時間不確定時，用來確認出發時間。
on time Fixed Expression
準時
The train is on time → 火車準時；公告中常和 delayed 對照。
get on Phrasal Verb
上車／登上
get on the train → 上火車；交通工具情境常用 get on。
miss the train Collocation
錯過火車
I do not want to miss the train → 我不想錯過火車。
running late Collocation
晚點；延誤中
The train is running late → 火車正在晚點；也可說 a bus is running late。
say that again more slowly Fixed Expression
再慢一點說一次
Could you say that again more slowly? → 聽不清楚時的 repair sentence。
platform change Collocation
月台變更
ask about a platform change → 詢問月台變更；看告示或 app 時可辨識。
