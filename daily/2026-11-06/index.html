單句模式

I'm at my hotel, looking at tickets for the viewing deck. I'd like to go on Monday, so I open the chat. Hi, can I use a weekday ticket on Monday? Yes, weekday tickets work from Monday to Friday. Great, does this ticket have a set entry time? Yes, pick an entry time when you book online. Can I choose two in the afternoon for Monday? The two o'clock slot's full, but we've still got space at two thirty. Sorry, which time can I still book? Two thirty on Monday.

Okay, I'll book the two thirty entry. I pick the later time and finish my booking. I've got my ticket number, but where's the ticket? Open your email and tap the link to your ticket. Can I download my ticket from that page? Sure, tap Download and save the file to your phone. Do I need to print it before I go? No, you can show it on your phone. Can I open the saved ticket offline?

Yes, once you've saved the file, it'll open offline. I turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data after saving the ticket. It opens offline, and I can see the whole ticket. Great, have it ready when you get to the entrance. Should I get there right at my entry time? Get there ten minutes before your entry time. So I'll get there at two twenty on Monday. That's right, then show your ticket at the entrance. Thanks, I'll save the arrival time in my calendar. You're all set, enjoy the view on Monday. I've got my ticket and know when to get there.
