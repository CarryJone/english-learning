Fixing a Missing Café Item
🎯 Mission
讀懂咖啡店取餐公告並處理缺少的餐點
你拿到外帶餐點後發現少了一項。先看取餐公告，再向店員說明問題、出示收據，並確認重做或替代方案。
成功條件：找出要出示收據的 Counter 2、重做需要十分鐘，以及餐點無法重做時的替代方案；最後說出缺少品項並提出修復請求。
英文輸入：café pickup notice + counter dialogue
🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行開口日常反應英文資訊判讀公共英文
今天的能力證據：Mission: fix a missing item in a café takeaway order. Input: pickup notice and counter dialogue. Find Counter 2, the ten-minute remake time, and the replacement/refund option. Say “I need to report a missing item.” / “Could you remake it?” / “I can show the receipt.”

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

Mina picks up her café order after a short trip. The pickup notice says, “Check each item before you leave.” If something is missing, show the receipt at Counter 2. The café can remake the item or give a refund. Mina checks the bag and sees the sandwich is missing.

She looks at the receipt and finds the order number. She says, “I need to report a missing item.” The staff checks the order and says, “We can replace it.” Mina asks, “Could you remake it?” The staff says, “Yes, but it will be ready in ten minutes.”

Mina asks, “Is there another option?” The staff says, “We can deliver the sandwich to your table.” Mina chooses the remake option and waits near the counter. She checks the bag again before leaving the café.

文章用法：look at the receipt → 查看收據上的訂單號碼或品項。
report a missing item Collocation

⭐ 今日開口目標：拿到餐點後發現少東西時，直接說明問題。
Could you remake it? Fixed Expression
可以重做這個嗎？
⭐ 今日修復目標：餐點少了、做錯或需要重新製作時使用。
another option Collocation
另一個方案；替代選擇
資訊改變時，用來詢問重做以外的選擇，例如替換或退款。
ready in ten minutes Fixed Expression

看到或聽到 ready in + 時間，就知道還要等多久。

💡 Learning Tips
  • 先從公告抓出 Counter 2、要出示收據，以及重做需要的時間；這三個資訊可以直接驗收任務。
  • 今天最值得主動說的是 I need to report a missing item. Could you remake it?；看到 show the receipt，就知道缺少餐點時要準備收據。
  • 如果原餐點現在無法重做，先問 Is there another option?，再確認替換、退款或稍後送到桌邊。
