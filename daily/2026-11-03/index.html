🎯 Mission
比較自行車租借費率，確認還車限制後再出發
在國外旅行，你想騎三小時逛河岸。先讀租借網頁，再向同一位店員確認費用、還車地點與時間。
✅ 比較三小時 £24 與日租 £18，選出較便宜方案；另付 £20 押金。
✅ 確認只可原店還車，最晚 17:30，不能把 18:00 關店當成還車時間。
✅ 能請店員調低座椅，再確認煞車。
英文輸入：租借網頁（教學用模擬，非真實店家條款）
RIVERSIDE BIKE RENTAL
Hourly rate: £8
Day rate: £18 — return by 17:30 today
Deposit: £20 — returned if the bike comes back undamaged
Helmet and lock included
Return location: same shop only
Shop hours: 09:00–18:00
今日開口句：What time should I bring it back?
🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行開口公共公告判讀即時澄清

先比較網頁的 hourly rate 與 day rate，再口頭確認還車截止時間；資訊聽不清楚時主動修復。

單句模式

I'm at a bike shop near the river. I've checked its rental page before coming in. Hi, what's the hourly rate for this bike? It's eight pounds, or eighteen for the day. I'd like to ride for three hours. So the day rate's cheaper for three hours? That's right, and a helmet's included too. Great, is there a deposit too? Yeah, it's twenty pounds, separate from the rental fee. When do I get my deposit back? When you're back, if there's no damage. Can I bring it back to another shop? Sorry, you need to bring it back here. Okay, what time should I bring it back today? We're open till six, but bring it back by five thirty, okay? Sorry, what time did you say for bringing it back? Five thirty, right here at this shop. Got it, I'll bring it back here by five thirty.

Before paying, I try the bike outside. This seat's too high for me. Can you adjust the seat for me, please? No problem, I'll move it down. Thanks, how do I change the gear? Use this button while you're turning the pedals. Which gear should I use on the small hill? Try a lower gear going up. Can I try it out before paying? Of course, and check the brake before you head out. It works, and the seat feels better. Does the price include a lock too? Yes, and this key opens it. I'll keep the key with me. Here's your helmet, and you're all set. Thanks, I'll be back by five thirty.

I've saved the return time on my phone.

💡 Learning Tips
主動背 What time should I bring it back? 與 Can you adjust the seat for me?；網頁先辨識 hourly rate，算出三小時 £24 比日租 £18 貴。
聽力裡 five thirty 是還車截止，six 是關店；遇到兩個時間先澄清，再把 17:30 存到手機提醒。
