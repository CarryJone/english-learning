Taking the Right City Bus During a Road Detour
播放速度
🎯 Mission
公車改道後，找到臨時站牌並在博物館下車
你要從手機地圖與公車站公告找出 Bus Twelve、臨時站牌的位置與 Museum Square；道路施工造成改道，你還要向工作人員確認路線、車程與下車站。
成功條件：找出公車改道、臨時站牌在書店對面、車程約十五分鐘與 Museum Square 三站後；說出 “Which bus goes to the museum?”、 “Where should I get off?”，並能在改道後找到正確站牌。
英文輸入：map result + bus detour notice + driver conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行開口公共英文英文資訊判讀日常反應
今天的能力證據：從地圖結果與改道公告找出 Bus Twelve、temporary stop、opposite the bookstore、Museum Square 與 three stops；用兩句目標語塊確認路線與下車位置。

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I leave my hotel after a quick breakfast. I want to visit the city museum before lunch. The map result says Bus Twelve goes there. I save the route on my phone before leaving. I walk to the bus stop near the busy market. The notice shows today's date and road work. It says Bus Twelve is on a detour. The temporary stop is opposite the bookstore. The notice shows a large blue arrow. I look across the street and find the blue arrow. I see the bus stop sign near a small bank.

The route number is a match for my saved result. Which bus goes to the museum? Bus Twelve goes to the museum. Is this the temporary stop? Yes. Please wait opposite the bookstore. How long does the full ride take? About fifteen minutes without traffic. Where should I get off? Get off at Museum Square. I repeat the stop name quietly in my head. The bus arrives from the other side of the street.

I show my travel pass to the driver. The driver points to the front door for boarding. I get on and find a seat. The road is busy near the market. We follow the detour around the old stone bridge. I watch the route on my phone during the ride. The map shows Museum Square after three short stops. I notice a large blue museum sign. I press the stop button near the museum square. I get off and check the building. The museum is opposite the city library. Now I can follow a detour and reach the city museum.

🔗 Key Phrases
which bus goes to the museum? Fixed Expression
哪一班公車會到博物館？
直接確認公車路線與目的地。
where should i get off? Fixed Expression
我應該在哪裡下車？
不知道下車站名時，直接向司機確認。
temporary stop Collocation
臨時站牌
公告或改道時常見的資訊；可替換成 temporary platform。
on a detour Collocation
正在繞道
The bus is on a detour → 交通改道時看公告、問工作人員都能用。
opposite the bookstore Collocation
在書店對面
用地標確認臨時站牌位置。
get off at Phrasal Verb
在……下車
Get off at Museum Square → 指定下車地點。
without traffic Fixed Expression
沒有塞車的情況下
About fifteen minutes without traffic → 估算車程。
three stops Fixed Expression
三站
The museum is three stops away → 從地圖或路線頁抓停靠站數。

💡 Learning Tips
  • 今天最值得主動說的是 Which bus goes to the museum? 和 Where should I get off?；一個確認路線，一個確認下車站。
  • 看到公告時，先抓 temporary stop、opposite the bookstore 和 on a detour；這些字能幫你找到改道後的站牌。
  • 車上再確認 three stops 和 Museum Square；聽不清楚時，先請對方重說，再決定在哪裡下車。
