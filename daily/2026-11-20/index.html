🎯 Mission
分食一份熱湯，讓兩個人各吃到喜歡的味道
旅客和朋友雨中散步後想吃熱食。朋友喜歡辣，她卻不太吃辣，所以點湯時先請店員分開放醬料，上餐後再要碗來分食。
✅ 從菜單確認大份湯附幾片麵包、加點麵包多少錢。
✅ 請店員把辣醬另外放，再拿一個碗和湯匙。
✅ 先分湯，再由朋友自己加辣醬，兩人都能享用。

單句模式

My friend and I stop at a café across from our hotel. After a wet walk, we check the menu display. Hi, we're thinking of sharing the soup and bread. Sure, the large soup comes with two slices of bread. That sounds good. Is the soup spicy? It's mild, but there's a hot sauce you can add. Could we have the sauce on the side? Of course. Would you like anything to drink? Just two glasses of water, please. No problem. Take a seat and we'll bring everything over.

We sit by the window and watch the rain. The worker sets down one big bowl and the bread. Here you go. Careful, the bowl's quite hot. Thanks. Could we have an extra bowl? We'd like a bowl each, if that's okay. Sure, I'll get you a small bowl and another spoon. Thanks, then we can each add what we like. You can add the hot sauce once you've split the soup. Good idea. My friend likes it much hotter than I do.

He brings the extra bowl, then points to a small pot. That's the hot sauce. Start with a little. I thought that was more soup! Careful, even a little can be quite hot. I'll leave that to my friend, then. By the way, you can get more bread for a pound at the counter. Sorry, how much is the extra bread? One pound for two slices. We'll have that too, please. Can I pay now? That's nine pounds altogether. You can tap right here. I pass my friend the bread while she adds the sauce. This is just what I needed after that walk. We finish lunch as the sun comes out over the street.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 點餐時用 on the side 請人分開放醬料。分食時用 Could we have an extra bowl?，再依需要加一支湯匙。
