Buying Sunscreen for a Beach Day
播放速度
🎯 Mission
看懂防曬標示，並買到適合海邊行程的小包裝
你要從藥妝店標示找出兩款防曬乳的 SPF、價格與補擦時間；確認敏感肌是否能用。小瓶缺貨時，要詢問適合旅行攜帶的替代品。
成功條件：找出 SPF 30／12 美元與 SPF 50／18 美元；說出 “How often should I reapply it?”；小瓶缺貨時選擇 10 美元的 SPF 50 防曬棒。
英文輸入：pharmacy shelf sign + sunscreen labels + short store conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行開口英文資訊判讀日常反應公共英文
今天的能力證據：從標籤找出 SPF 30／50、$12／$18、forty／eighty minutes 與 reapply；用 “Is this okay for sensitive skin?” 確認適用性，小瓶售罄時用 “Do you have a smaller bottle?” 找替代品。

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I leave my hotel for a sunny beach day. The sun is strong this morning. I stop at a small pharmacy one block away. Near the door, a shelf sign says “Travel Sun Care.” It shows two bottles of sunscreen for travelers. The first bottle is SPF thirty. It costs twelve dollars for a large bottle. The label says “Water resistant: forty minutes.” It also says “Reapply after swimming.” The second bottle is SPF fifty. It costs eighteen dollars and has a blue label. Its label says “For sensitive skin.” It is water resistant for eighty minutes. I plan to swim and walk along the beach. I need strong sun care for the whole afternoon. I pick up the SPF fifty bottle. Then I read the directions on the back. They say “Reapply every two hours.”

The words are simple, but I want to check. How often should I reapply it? Please reapply it every two hours. Also use it again after swimming. Is this okay for sensitive skin? Yes, this one has no added perfume. I do not know the word perfume. Could you say that more slowly? It has no perfume, so the smell is light. Now the label is easier to understand.

I want the smaller option for my day bag.

Do you have a smaller bottle? Sorry, the small bottle is sold out. We have a travel-size stick instead. This item costs ten dollars and is SPF fifty. Is it okay for sensitive skin too? Yes, and reapply it after swimming. Great, I’ll take the travel-size stick.

🔗 Key Phrases
pick up Phrasal Verb
拿起；拿取
pick up the SPF fifty bottle → 拿起 SPF 50 的瓶子。
sold out Phrasal Verb
售罄；賣完
the small bottle is sold out → 小瓶已賣完。
water resistant Collocation
抗水的；防潑水的
標籤資訊：可抗水四十或八十分鐘。
sensitive skin Collocation
敏感肌膚
For sensitive skin 是包裝上常見說法。
after swimming Fixed Expression
游泳後
標籤指示你游泳後要再擦一次。
How often should I reapply it? Fixed Expression · 今日目標
我應該多久補擦一次？
看懂 reapply 後，用這句確認補擦頻率。
Is this okay for sensitive skin? Fixed Expression · 今日目標
這適合敏感肌嗎？
買保養品或防曬用品時直接詢問。
Do you have a smaller bottle? Fixed Expression · 今日目標
你們有小一點的瓶子嗎？
購物時詢問更適合旅行攜帶的尺寸。
Could you say that more slowly? Fixed Expression
可以說慢一點嗎？
沒聽懂產品說明時，用這句請對方放慢。
