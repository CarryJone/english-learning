🎯 Mission
插座沒電時，回報問題並借行動電源
回到飯店後手機無法充電。先打電話回報床邊插座問題，再下樓報房號、借行動電源，回房間暫時充電並等待人員檢查。
✅ 說明同一插座上的手機與檯燈都沒有供電。
✅ 向同一位接待員報房號，借到可帶回房間的行動電源。
✅ 確認明早十點前須親自還到櫃檯。
英文輸入為模擬飯店借用說明
PHONE CHARGING HELP
Room socket not working? Contact reception.
Power banks available to borrow at the front desk.
Use your own charging cable.
Bring the power bank back to reception by 10 a.m. the next morning.
Please hand it to a staff member.

開口完成任務：The socket by my bed doesn't work. Can I borrow a power bank?

🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行求助借用說明判讀

回報插座問題，面談時接上剛才的電話，確認行動電源的借用、充電方式與歸還期限。

單句模式

I'm back in my hotel room after a long day. My phone isn't charging, so I call reception for help. Hi, the socket by my bed doesn't work. Sorry about that, does your charger work anywhere else? Yes, it worked at the airport this morning. Is the lamp plugged into that same socket working? No, the lamp's off too, so there's no power there. Thanks, we'll send someone up to check that socket. What can I do to charge my phone tonight? Come down to reception, we've got a power bank to lend.

I go downstairs to speak to the same receptionist. Hi, I'm from room twelve, we just spoke on the phone. Yes, here's the power bank we talked about. Can I borrow a power bank for my room, please? Of course, just connect your charging cable to it. Great, then I won't need the room socket to charge. You can borrow it overnight, but we'll need it back by ten tomorrow. Sorry, what time do you need the power bank back? By ten tomorrow morning, here at reception.

I connect my phone and check that it's charging. It's charging now, thanks for helping me out. No problem, we'll let you know when we've checked the socket. Should I contact reception if the socket still doesn't work? Yes, call us from your room and we'll help. Can I leave the power bank in the drawer tomorrow? Please bring it back here, so we know you've returned it. Okay, I'll bring it back to this desk tomorrow morning. Great, take the power bank with you to your room. Thanks, I can charge my phone while someone checks the socket. I've got power for my phone while I wait for help.
