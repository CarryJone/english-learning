單句模式

I check online for an easy walk before lunch. I find two walks and open the help chat. Hi, I'm comparing the riverside and hill walks. Sure, what kind of walk would you like? Something easy, with time to stop for photos. The riverside walk sounds like a good choice. The page says flat path, so there aren't any hills? That's right, it's flat, with seats along the way. And the hill walk has steep steps, is that right? Yeah, and the group walks at quite a fast pace.

I compare both options before choosing my walk. Can I walk at my own pace on the riverside route? Sure, it's an audio walk for your phone. Great, I won't need to keep up with a group. Exactly, stop and start whenever you like. It says bring your own headphones, so mine will work? Yes, use headphones that work with your phone. Okay, and is the riverside walk about forty-five minutes long? Yes, it'll take longer with stops for photos. I'll choose that one for six pounds. Good choice, there's a cafe nearby for lunch.

Before I pay, I ask where the audio walk starts. It starts by the library today, not the fountain, if you're coming now. Sorry, where does the walk start today? By the library, not the fountain. The library, got it, and can I start there at eleven? Sure, you can start any time before five today. Great, I'll save the map on my phone before leaving. That's a good idea, and check your phone's charged too. Thanks, I'll bring my own headphones and walk at my own pace. You're welcome, enjoy your walk and take your time. I've chosen my walk and checked the starting point.
