單句模式

I'm at a bakery across from my hotel. I want some bread for a picnic in the park. Hi, can I have a loaf of bread, please? Sure, what kind of bread would you like today? I'd like the cheese loaf on the board, please. Sorry, we're out of cheese bread, but we've still got the plain loaf. Sorry, which loaf have you still got? The plain loaf. Okay, is the plain loaf three pounds like the sign says? That's right, and there's a small loaf in this basket.

I look at the loaf and think about our picnic. I'll take that plain loaf for our picnic, please. Great, would you like me to slice it for you? Yes, please, is there an extra charge for that? No, we'll slice it for free. Great, please slice the whole loaf for our picnic. Could you make the slices thin, please? Sure, I'll cut it into thin slices for sandwiches. Thanks, can you put it in a paper bag too? Sure, I'll put it in a bag for you.

I take out my card while the baker packs the bread. Can I pay by card for just one loaf? Of course, you can pay by card for any amount. So it's three pounds with the slicing and the bag? Yes, it's three pounds in total. Great, I'll pay by card, please. Just tap your card here when you're ready. Thanks, can I have the receipt as well? Sure, here's your receipt and your bag. Thanks, that's everything I need for the picnic. I leave with the sliced bread and head to the park.
