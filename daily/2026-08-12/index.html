Choosing a Safe Hotel Breakfast

A2 · Short-Sentence Travel Mission

🎧 2-Minute A2 Dual-Voice Practice
Choosing a Safe Hotel Breakfast
播放速度
🎯 Mission
看懂飯店早餐資訊，並安全處理食物過敏與缺餐
你要從早餐菜單找出供應時間、成人價格與續杯規則；點餐時確認食物是否含堅果。原本想吃的蛋臨時沒有時，要詢問安全的替代品。
成功條件：找出平日 7:00–10:00、成人 12 美元與咖啡／茶可免費續杯；說出 “Does this contain nuts?”；蛋沒有時改點不含堅果的燕麥粥。
英文輸入：hotel breakfast menu + allergy warning + short cafe conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行開口英文資訊判讀日常反應公共英文
今天的能力證據：從菜單找出 breakfast buffet、7:00–10:00、$12 與 free refills；用 “Does this contain nuts?” 確認過敏原，餐點缺貨時用 “What can I have instead?” 找安全替代品。

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I stay at a small hotel near the main station. Breakfast is not included because I booked room only. I check the printed menu near the cafe. It says, “Breakfast Buffet, seven to ten on weekdays.” Adults pay twelve dollars, and children pay six. Coffee and tea have free refills during breakfast. A printed warning appears at the bottom in red. “Nut allergy? Please tell staff before ordering.” I have a nut allergy and carry a card. I read the warning one more time. Then I walk into the hotel cafe.

Good morning. Are you ready to order? Yes. I’d like the breakfast buffet, please. Of course. Would you like coffee or tea? Coffee, please. Does it have free refills? Yes, coffee and tea have free refills. I also want some banana cake. Does this contain nuts? Yes. The banana cake contains walnuts. Thank you for telling me. Which foods do not contain nuts? The eggs, toast, and fruit are safe. I choose eggs, toast, and fresh fruit. The worker gives me a clean plate.

A few minutes later, she returns.

Sorry, we have no eggs right now. I understand, but I still need breakfast. What can I have instead? We can offer oatmeal or extra fruit. Oatmeal is a good substitute for eggs. Does the oatmeal contain nuts? No. We make it without nuts. Great. I’ll have oatmeal instead. The worker brings coffee and oatmeal.

I enjoy a safe breakfast before sightseeing. I ask for one coffee refill before leaving.

💡 Learning Tips

  • 有過敏時說 Does this contain nuts?；餐點沒有時接 What can I have instead?。
