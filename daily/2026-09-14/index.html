Picking Seats Together on a Full Flight
播放速度
🎯 Mission
讀懂線上報到規則，兩個人坐在一起
明天要飛，你今晚線上報到。要從報到頁找出免費選位的時間、前排加價金額與行李額度；走道位被選完、你和同行的人被分開坐時，先打電話問，再到機場櫃檯換位。
成功條件：找出幾小時前開始免費選位、前排要多付多少、託運行李幾公斤；說出「要另外收費嗎」與「我們可以換位子嗎」兩句英文，並在沒聽清楚時問回「要等到什麼時候」。
英文輸入：航空公司線上報到頁 + 座位圖 + 客服電話
Online check-in · CX 402 · Hong Kong → Taipei
Seat selection: free of charge from 48 hours before departure
Front rows (1–10): extra fee US$25
More seats are released at airport check-in
Checked bag: 1 × 23 kg included
Boarding: Gate 68 · 15:45

單句模式

Our flight's tomorrow, so we check in online tonight. I open the airline app and my friend leans over. Does it cost anything to pick a seat? It says seat selection's free of charge from now. Nice. Grab two seats together. The map's loading... okay, here's the cabin. Which row are we in? Row 14, but only middle seats are left. No aisle seats at all? Nope. Row 31's got one window seat. We'd be split up, though. And there's an extra fee for the front rows.

I call the airline to see what we can do. Thanks for holding. How can I help? We're on CX 402 tomorrow, and we can't sit together. Let me pull up your booking. One moment. Right, you're both in middle seats in Row 14. Is there an extra fee to change them? Not at the airport, no. It's free at the desk. So we shouldn't pay online? We release more seats at check-in, free of charge, so wait. Sorry, I didn't catch that. Wait until when? Until you check in at the airport. Got it. Thanks so much.

The next day we get to the desk early. Hi, could we swap seats? We're in Row 14. Let me see what's free. There's an aisle and a window in Row 22. We'll take those. Are they together? Yep, 22C and 22A, with one seat between. Could we swap that middle one too? It's empty, so just move over after boarding. We end up with an aisle and a window. No extra fee at all? Nope. Free of charge. Next time I'll check in the moment it opens.

🔗 Key Phrases
free of charge Collocation今日目標
免費，不另外收錢
報到頁、訂房頁、退換規則都會出現；看到它就代表不用加錢。
Could we swap seats? Fixed Expression今日目標
我們可以換位子嗎？
飛機、火車、餐廳都能用；一個人講就是 Could I swap seats?
Is there an extra fee? Fixed Expression今日目標
要另外收費嗎？
改時間、加行李、換房型前先問這句，免得結帳才發現。
check in online Phrasal Verb
線上報到
check in 也用在飯店入住；名詞寫成 check-in，中間有連字號。
sit together Collocation
坐在一起
we can't sit together 是換位的理由，講出來對方比較好幫忙。
end up with Phrasal Verb
最後拿到；最後變成
講結果很好用：I ended up with a middle seat.（最後只拿到中間位）

💡 Learning Tips
  • 今天最值得背起來的是 Could we swap seats?；飛機、火車、餐廳都能用，一個人講就改成 Could I swap seats?
  • Is there an extra fee? 要養成在動作之前先問的習慣：改時間、加行李、換房型，先問這句就不會結帳才嚇一跳。
  • 小提醒：free 今天有兩個意思——free of charge（免費）和 Let me see what's free（空著沒人用）。看到 free 先判斷是「不用錢」還是「沒人坐」。
