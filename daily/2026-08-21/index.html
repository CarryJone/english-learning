Returning a Rental Car with a Full Tank

A2 · Short-Sentence Travel Mission

🎧 2-Minute A2 Dual-Voice Practice
Returning a Rental Car with a Full Tank
播放速度
🎯 Mission
讀懂租車還車規則，確認加油、停車區與押金退回
你要從租車確認信、還車告示與停車場指示找出還車時間、油量要求、指定區域和押金處理方式；再處理 B 區客滿的臨時改道。
成功條件：找出晚上 8 點前還車、油箱要滿、使用 B 區後改到 C 區；說出 “Where do I return the car?” 與 “Does the tank need to be full?”；保留收據並確認押金退回。
英文輸入：rental confirmation + fuel policy + parking notice + rental-desk conversations
🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行開口線上資訊判讀公共英文日常反應
今天的能力證據：從確認信抓出 8 p.m.、full tank 與 Lot B；用 “Where do I return the car?”、 “Does the tank need to be full?” 現場確認，再依 Lot B full 的告示改到 Lot C。

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I open my rental car confirmation before leaving. The email says, “Return the car by eight p.m.” It also says, “Use the airport return lot.” I check the map and save Lot B. A note says, “Return the car with a full tank.” I read the fuel policy on the company website. It says I must keep the last gas receipt. The rental counter closes at eight thirty tonight. I save the counter address on my phone. I also check my parking pass and room key. A return notice says, “Press the blue button at the gate.” Then take a ticket and follow the orange signs.

At the rental counter, I show my booking and passport. “Where do I return the car?” “Use Lot B, then walk to the rental counter.” “Does the tank need to be full?” “Yes, please fill up the tank before entering the lot.” “What should I do if I find new damage?” “Please describe the damage and take clear photos.” “Do I need the gas receipt?” “Yes. Put it with the keys in the return box.” “Can I pay the final amount with cash?” “The deposit returns to your card after inspection.”
I drive toward the airport and follow the orange signs. A new notice says, “Lot B is full this evening.” It sends returning cars to Lot C beside the main terminal. I tap the sign and read the new return steps. The new map says the counter moved beside Gate Two. I ask a staff member for help with the change. “Can I return the car in Lot C tonight?” “Yes. Press the green button and keep the printed receipt.” I park carefully, return the keys, and wait for the deposit.

🔗 Key Phrases
fill up Phrasal Verb · 今日目標
加滿（油、水等）
fill up the tank → 把油箱加滿；看到 fuel policy 時很常見。
rental counter Collocation
租車櫃檯
walk to the rental counter → 走到租車櫃檯。
full tank Collocation
滿油箱
return the car with a full tank → 還車時油箱要滿。
take clear photos Collocation · 今日目標
拍清楚的照片
take clear photos of damage → 拍下損傷，方便交車時說明。
where do I return the car? Fixed Expression · 今日目標
我應該在哪裡還車？
換成 bike、keys 或 ticket 也能問不同歸還地點。
does the tank need to be full? Fixed Expression · 今日目標
油箱需要是滿的嗎？
先確認規則，再決定是否要在入口前加油。

💡 Learning Tips
