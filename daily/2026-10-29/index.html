🎯 Mission
房卡打不開門，說明狀況並確認下一步
回到旅館卻無法進房。讀房卡操作說明，向同一位櫃檯人員描述提示音與紅燈，確認重設、備用卡與求助方式。
✅ 找出感應位置與綠燈後的動作。
✅ 確認重設不收額外費用，取得可用的備用房卡。
✅ 若再次失敗，知道從電梯旁電話聯絡櫃檯。
英文輸入：房卡操作與服務說明（教學用模擬；不同旅館設備可能不同）
ROOM KEY HELP
Tap card against the reader above the handle.
Green light: push the handle down.
Red light: contact reception.
Card reset: no extra charge.
Need help upstairs? Use the phone next to the lift.

開口完成任務：My key card doesn't work.

🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行求助公共說明判讀

描述房卡故障、讀懂操作步驟與費用提示，聽不清楚時請對方重述，再確認再次失敗的求助方式。

單句模式

I'm back at my hotel, but my door won't open. I take my key card down to reception. Hi, my key card doesn't work on my room door. Sorry about that, what happens when you try it? There's a beep, and the light flashes red. Okay, let's check your card and get you in. Do I tap it or put it in? Just tap it against the reader above the handle. Right, I did that, but I still couldn't get in. Let me check the exact room number first.

I show my ID and point to my room number. It's room twelve, and I'm staying two more nights. Thanks, I'll reset this card for your stay. Is there an extra charge to reset it? No, there's no extra charge for resetting this card. Great, can I have a spare key card as well? Sure, I'll make you a spare while you're here. Will my first card still work after you do that? Yes, both cards will work for your room. How long should I hold it against the reader? Hold it there till the green light comes on, then push the handle down. Sorry, what do I do when the light turns green? Push the handle down when it's green.

Before heading upstairs, I check the next step. Okay, I'll try again with this card. Good, keep your spare somewhere safe. What if I hear a beep but it flashes red again? Call reception from the phone next to the lift. Can someone come up and help me at the door? Of course, we'll send someone up to help you. Thanks, I've got both cards and know what to try. I've checked the steps and know how to get help.
