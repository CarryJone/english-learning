🎯 Mission
拿到錯的飲料，核對收據並請店員更換
你在公車站旁的咖啡店點了外帶熱茶，店員卻給了冰檸檬茶。用收據說明原本點的飲料，再確認等待時間與外帶杯蓋。
✅ 用收據說明你已付 £2，點的是原味熱茶。
✅ 請店員收回冰茶，確認約三分鐘可換成熱茶。
✅ 確認有杯蓋，拿著熱茶回站牌搭車。
英文輸入為模擬咖啡店菜單與收據
CAFÉ MENU
Plain hot tea — £2
Iced lemon tea — £3
To go: lids available on request.
YOUR RECEIPT
1 plain hot tea, to go
Paid by card — £2

開口完成任務。I ordered a plain hot tea to go. Can you take it back?

單句模式

I'm at a café beside the bus shelter. I've got ten minutes before my bus leaves. Can I have a plain hot tea to go? Sure, would you like any lemon with that? No, thanks, I'd like it plain, just hot tea. Okay, that'll be two pounds. Thanks, I'll pay by card. Here's your receipt, just wait by the counter.

The same worker hands me a cup of iced tea. Excuse me, I think this is the iced lemon tea. Oh, you're right, let me check your receipt. I ordered a plain hot tea to go. That's what the receipt says, sorry about that. Can you take this back and make a hot tea? Of course, I'll take it back and make yours now. I've got a bus to catch, how long will it take? I'll make you a fresh cup of hot tea, but it'll take three minutes. Sorry, how many minutes did you say? Three minutes. Great, I've got time, so I'll wait here. And could you make it without lemon, please? Of course, just plain hot tea.

I wait by the counter while the worker makes my tea. Here you go, one plain hot tea to go. Could you put a lid on the cup? Sure, I'll put a lid on it now. Thanks, it'll be easier to carry to the bus. Thanks, do I need to pay anything else? No, you've already paid for the hot tea. Great, I'll take it with me to the bus stop. You're welcome, have a good trip. I check the lid and pick up my hot tea. I head to the shelter before my bus leaves.
