🎯 Mission
在車站找回媽媽送的圍巾
旅客離開車站才發現圍巾不見了。她回頭找到失物招領處，描述外觀，拿出照片，並用圍巾內側的名字確認是自己的。
✅ 從告示找出失物招領處的位置，以及有什麼資料可幫忙辨認。
✅ 描述圍巾顏色、條紋和材質，再說出標籤上的名字。
✅ 確認物品並簽名領回，再戴上圍巾離開。

單句模式

I'm leaving the station when I reach for my scarf. It's gone, and it's the one Mum gave me. I turn back and spot a Lost Property sign. Hi, has anyone handed in a scarf this afternoon? A few people have. What does yours look like? It's blue and green, and it's made of wool. Okay, where do you think you left it? On a bench near the screen with the train times. Right, let me have a look in the box here.

I've got a photo of myself wearing it, if that helps. Yes, that would be useful. Can you show me? I open a photo from the first day of my trip. Here, I'm wearing it in front of the museum. I've got a blue one here, with thin green stripes at both ends. Sorry, what colour are the stripes? Green stripes on a blue scarf. That sounds like mine. It's a long striped scarf. Before I show you, is there anything else I can check? My name's on the label, in Mum's handwriting. She put it there because I always leave things behind. What's the name, please? It's Lily Chen. The writing's quite small.

He checks one end, then turns the scarf over. Yes, there it is. Lily Chen. Oh, I'm so glad. I thought I'd lost it for good. Just write your name here to say you've picked it up. Of course. Can I use this pen? Go ahead. Here you are, and keep warm out there. Thanks for looking. Mum will be pleased too. I put the scarf on before I leave the station. Outside, I send Mum a photo with it safely round my neck.
