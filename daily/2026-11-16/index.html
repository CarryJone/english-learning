🎯 Mission
在海港紀念品店寄出明信片和照片
旅客看到一張有港口照片的明信片，想到以前在船上工作的爸爸。她想連同小照片一起寄回台灣。
✅ 從價目表找出海外明信片與一般信件的郵寄價格。
✅ 確認照片要放進信封，算出一般郵寄的總價。
✅ 付費後把信封交給店員，讓店員貼郵票寄出。
英文輸入為模擬紀念品店價目表
MAIL FROM THIS SHOP
Overseas postcards: £2
Overseas letters (Taiwan included), standard mail: £2.50
Small envelope: 50p
Leave your mail at the till.

開口完成任務。I'd like to send it to Taiwan.

單句模式

I pick up a postcard in a shop by the harbour. There's a little fishing boat on it, like Dad's old boat. I want to send Dad a little surprise from my trip. Hi, do you sell stamps here too? We do, and we can mail your card from here. Great, I'd like to send it to Taiwan. Sure, there's a price list right by the till. It says two pounds for postcards going overseas. That's right, two pounds will get it to Taiwan. I'll take a stamp for the card, please.

I take out a photo of myself on the harbour bridge. Can I send this photo with the card, too? Oh, you'll need an envelope for that. I was going to tape it to the back. It could fall off. This size should fit both the photo and the card. How much will it cost with the envelope? With the envelope, it'll be three pounds altogether if you use standard mail. Sorry, how much did you say? Three pounds in total.

That works for me, thanks. Here's a pen if you want to write him a note. Thanks, I'll tell him this boat made me think of him. I'll put his address here on the front. I put the card and photo inside and seal the envelope. There we go. Can I pay by card? Of course, you can tap right here. When will it go out? Our mail goes out this afternoon. I'll put the right stamp on for you. Thanks, I hope he gets it before I get home. I hand over the envelope and watch the stamp go on.
