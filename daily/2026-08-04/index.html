🎧 3-Minute Listening Practice
Choosing an Airport Ride After a Stop Change
🎯 Mission
比較機場交通並處理接駁車停靠站變更
你剛抵達國外機場，要從官方交通頁比較市區火車與旅館接駁車，再向服務台確認時間與上車地點；當站牌臨時變更時，找出新停靠站並完成預訂。
成功條件：找出兩種交通的票價、車程與出發點；選出適合方案；說出 “How long does it take?” 與 “Where does it leave from?”，並依更新前往 Door 4 附近的 Stop 8。
英文輸入：airport transport page + information-desk dialogue + service update
🗺️ Ability Focus
英文資訊判讀旅行開口公共英文日常反應
今天的能力證據：Mission: compare airport transport and handle a changed shuttle stop. Input: transport page, information-desk dialogue, and service update. Find both fares, travel times, and departure points. Say “How long does it take?” / “Where does it leave from?” / “Could you say that again?”
📖 Today's Article
今日單字
複習單字
重要片語
🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I arrive at Harbor City Airport at nine in the evening. My hotel is downtown, so I need a simple route. I look up airport transport on my phone. The page says, “Compare the options before you travel.” I see the City Train and the Hotel Shuttle. The train leaves from Terminal One, Level B One. It runs every fifteen minutes until eleven thirty. The one-way fare is twelve dollars for each passenger. Its travel time is about thirty-five minutes. The shuttle leaves from Stop Six outside the arrivals hall. It runs every thirty minutes until midnight. Its fare is eight dollars, including one large suitcase. The shuttle travel time is about fifty minutes. I compare both choices and write down each important detail.

The train is faster, but it costs four dollars more. The shuttle is cheaper and stops near my hotel. I choose the shuttle because my suitcase feels very heavy. Before booking, I check the destination name carefully. The page says, “Central Hotel District,” not my hotel name. I cannot tell whether the shuttle stops close enough. I walk to the airport information desk for help. Me: “Excuse me, how long does it take?” Staff: “It usually takes about fifty minutes.” Me: “Where does it leave from?” Staff: “It leaves from Stop Six, outside the arrivals hall.” I repeat the place and time to make sure. “Stop Six, outside arrivals, and every thirty minutes.” The staff confirms everything, so I open the booking page. I enter my destination and select the ten o'clock shuttle.

The page is working, but a red update appears. “Stop Six is closed tonight because of road work.” “Shuttles now leave from Stop Eight near Door Four.” My booking has not expired, but it shows the old stop. I do not want to wait at the wrong place. Me: “Sorry, where does it leave from now?” Staff: “Please use Stop Eight near Door Four.” I miss the door number because the airport is noisy. Me: “Could you say that again, please?” Staff: “Door Four, then turn right at the corner.” I update the pickup point and check the duration again. The new route takes sixty minutes, but the fare stays eight dollars. I compare the options again and keep my shuttle booking. At Stop Eight, I confirm the destination with the driver. I board the correct shuttle and complete my travel mission.

用來判讀班距；不是只在三十分鐘後出發一次。

🎭 Role-play

你在機場服務台比較交通並確認接駁車。先遮住 You 的句子再開口；當 Stop 6 關閉時，改問新的上車地點。

Staff
The City Train takes thirty-five minutes and costs twelve dollars.
You
I want to compare the options first.
Staff
The hotel shuttle costs eight dollars.
You
How long does it take?
Staff
It takes fifty minutes, but Stop Six is closed tonight.
資訊改變：原本的 Stop 6 關閉，接駁車改到另一個停靠站。
You
Where does it leave from now?
Staff
Stop Eight near Door Four.
You
Sorry, did you say Door Four or Door Five?
Staff
Door Four. Turn right at the corner.
You
Thank you. I will keep my shuttle booking.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 先從交通頁抓出三組資訊：票價、車程、出發點；這三項就是比較交通選項的任務成功條件。
  • 今天最值得主動說的是 How long does it take? 與 Where does it leave from?；看到 one-way fare 與 duration，就能快速讀懂票價和時間。
  • 站點臨時改變時，先再問一次出發地點；若沒聽清楚門號，就說 Could you say that again?。
