Fixing a Broken Rental Bike
🎯 Mission
處理租借腳踏車的故障並換車
你租到的腳踏車鎖壞了。先讀租借 App 的提示，再向服務人員說明問題、找到替代車並完成掃碼。
成功條件：找出 Bike 18 的問題、替代車所在的 Station 3、騎乘前要掃描 code，以及晚上 10 點前的還車時間；最後說出鎖壞了並要求換車。
英文輸入：租借 App notice + help desk dialogue
🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行開口日常反應英文資訊判讀公共英文
今天的能力證據：Mission: report a broken rental-bike lock and get a replacement. Input: bike-rental app notice and help-desk dialogue. Find Bike 18's problem, the replacement-bike station, the scan instruction, and the 10 p.m. return time. Say “The lock is broken. Can you help me?” / “Could you say that again?” / “Please scan the code before you ride.”

單句模式

The app says Bike 18 is affected by a lock problem. The lock is broken, so do not ride it. Please choose a replacement bike at Station 3. Scan the code before you ride. Return the bike by 10 p.m.

Mina checks Bike 18 near the station. The lock does not open. She says, “I need to report a broken lock.” The staff member says, “We can replace the bike.” Mina asks, “Could you say that again?”

The staff member repeats, “Station 3, near the blue sign.” Mina asks, “What should I do now?” The staff says, “Pick up the replacement bike and scan the code.” Mina chooses a new bike and returns it by 10 p.m.

🔗 Key Phrases
report a broken lock Collocation
通報鎖壞了
文章用法：report a broken lock → 向服務人員通報鎖壞了；遇到設備故障也可以換成 report a broken screen。
choose a replacement bike Collocation
選一台替代腳踏車
在 App 或服務人員說明中，表示選一台替代車；也可以換成 choose a replacement bus。
Could you say that again? Fixed Expression
你可以再說一次嗎？
聽不清楚站點或號碼時，請對方重說；比只說 What? 更完整。
pick up Phrasal Verb
取走；取車
pick up a replacement bike → 到指定站點取替代車。
scan the code Collocation
掃描代碼
租借 App 或設備流程常見說法，掃描後才開始騎乘。
return the bike by 10 p.m. Fixed Expression
晚上十點前還車
看到 by + 時間，就是「不晚於這個時間」完成還車。

先遮住參考答案，依序說出你的回應。第三或第四回合必須處理資訊改變、聽不懂、澄清或替代方案。

Hello. What is the problem?
You
The lock is broken. Can you help me?
Staff
Bike 18 is affected by a lock problem. Please choose a replacement bike at Station 3.
You
I need to report a broken lock. Could you say that again?
Staff
Sorry, Station 3 is full now. Choose a replacement bike at Station 4 instead.
資訊改變：Station 3 額滿，替代車改到 Station 4。
You
Okay. I will choose a replacement bike at Station 4. What should I do now?
Staff
Pick up the replacement bike and scan the code before you ride.
You
Okay. I will scan the code before I ride and return the bike by 10 p.m.
Staff
Good. Please report another problem at the help desk.
