Fixing a Declined Card Payment at the Station
播放速度
🎯 Mission
售票機刷卡失敗時，改到櫃檯買機場快線車票
讀懂售票機錯誤提示和車站指示，確認是否扣款、哪裡能改用其他方式付款，再買對單程票並找到正確時間與月台。
完成條件：指出付款遭拒且未扣款；找到 Ticket Desk 4 的營業時間與可用付款方式；確認 Airport Express 單程票價格、開車時間和月台；向店員說出「Can I pay in cash instead?」

單句模式

I'm at Riverside Station, and I need a ticket to the airport. The ticket kiosk is right by the entrance. My card was declined at the kiosk. It says, “PAYMENT DECLINED. NO MONEY WAS TAKEN.” I can try another way to pay before my train. A paper sign points to Ticket Desk 4 beside the exit. Two people are waiting, but the line moves quickly.

Excuse me, is Desk 4 open right now? Yes, we've got until ten tonight. Can I buy a single ticket to the airport? Sure. Are you taking the Airport Express tonight? Yes, I'd like the next one, please. It leaves at eight forty from Platform 6. Thanks. Is that the train by the blue sign? That's right. The blue sign says Airport Express. I check the sign and get my wallet ready. Can I pay in cash instead? Even if the kiosk declines your card, we can take cash at Desk 4. Sorry, did you say cash is okay here? Yes. Cash is fine at Desk 4. I take out a twenty-dollar bill and check the exact total. Here's twenty. Could I get some change? Of course. The ticket's eighteen dollars. Here's your ticket and your receipt. It says Platform 6 and eight forty, right? Yes, and the platform's just up those stairs. How long does it take to get there? About five minutes if you follow the blue signs.

I put the ticket and receipt away. The kiosk's still not working, but I've got a plan. I can still get on the right train. I head upstairs and check the departure screen. The airport train's still on time. I keep my ticket in my hand.
