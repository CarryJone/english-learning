Ordering a Safe Café Meal with a Food Allergy

A2 · Short-Sentence Travel Mission

🎧 2-Minute A2 Three-Voice Practice
Ordering a Safe Café Meal with a Food Allergy
播放速度
🎯 Mission
看懂餐廳菜單並處理食物過敏
你要在海外飯店咖啡廳用手機看菜單，確認餐點是否含乳製品、附哪些配菜，再向店員完成點餐；原本的雞肉飯碗售罄後，你還要改選替代餐點。
成功條件：找出雞肉飯碗 14 美元、豆腐飯碗 13 美元（未含稅）、菜餚不含乳製品且附飯和蔬菜；說出 “Is this dish dairy-free?”、 “What does it come with?”、 “Is there another option?”，並能在聽不清楚時請對方重說。
英文輸入：café menu + allergy notice + ordering conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
英文資訊判讀旅行開口公共英文日常反應
今天的能力證據：從手機菜單與過敏提示找出 dairy-free、14 dollars、rice and vegetables、sold out、13 dollars before tax；用三個目標語塊確認成分、附餐與替代方案，並用 say that again 處理聽不清楚。

🎙️ 聲線提示：旁白＝場景與動作；旅客＝我的提問；工作人員＝指示與回答。

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I enter the hotel café before my morning tour. I need a quick meal before I leave. I open the café menu on my phone. The lunch menu has three main dishes. The chicken rice bowl costs fourteen dollars. It is marked as a dairy-free dish. The note says, "Please ask about ingredients." Meals come with rice and vegetables. I check the small list under each item. One ingredient is missing from the short note. The chicken bowl has a food allergy mark. I want to make sure the information is clear. I walk to the counter and wait.

Is this dish dairy-free? Yes, this dish contains no dairy. What does it come with? It comes with rice and steamed vegetables. Can I get the sauce on the side? Yes, we can put the sauce on the side. Great. I will order the chicken rice bowl. I will also have a bottle of water.

The cashier checks the screen. The chicken item is sold out today. I am sorry, but that item is no longer available. I take a breath and check the other menu items. Is there another option? The tofu bowl is available, but it has a spicy sauce. Does it come with the same vegetables? Yes, and we can leave the sauce on the side. Could you say that again, please? Of course. It is the tofu rice bowl. I choose that option and confirm the price. The tofu bowl is thirteen dollars, before tax. I repeat the order slowly before paying. This dish contains no dairy, and sauce is on the side. Now I can order safely before my tour.

🔗 Key Phrases
Is this dish dairy-free? Fixed Expression
這道菜不含乳製品嗎？
今日目標語塊：點餐前直接確認食物是否適合自己。
come with Phrasal Verb
附帶；包含
今日目標語塊：What does it come with? 用來問套餐或菜餚附什麼。
another option Collocation
另一個選擇
今日目標語塊：原本的餐點沒有了，就用 Is there another option? 問替代方案。
on the side Fixed Expression
另外放；分開放
請店家把醬料或配料另外放，不要直接混在主餐裡。
sold out Fixed Expression
售罄；賣完了
菜單、商店或售票頁看到 sold out，就是目前不能再買。
say that again Fixed Expression
再說一次
沒聽清楚菜名或價格時，用 Could you say that again? 禮貌澄清。
contains no dairy Collocation
不含乳製品
菜單或店員說明食物成分時常見的資訊語塊。
before tax Fixed Expression
未含稅；稅前
看到價格時確認 before tax，才不會把未含稅價格當成最後金額。

💡 Learning Tips
  • 今天最值得主動說的是 Is this dish dairy-free?；有飲食限制時，先確認再點餐。
  • 看到菜單時，先抓 come with、contains no dairy 和 before tax；它們分別告訴你附餐、成分與價格是否含稅。
  • 雞肉飯碗售罄時，先說 Is there another option?；聽不清楚菜名或價格，就接 Could you say that again, please?。
