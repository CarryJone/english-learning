Booking a Dinner Table After Reading Reviews

Booking a Dinner Table After Reading Reviews
播放速度
🎯 Mission
讀懂評論與訂位規則，訂到今晚的位子
你要用手機查旅館附近的餐廳，先從評論找出訂位建議與座位提醒，再從訂位頁確認免費取消期限與保留時間；七點半訂滿時，改訂別的時段並指定室內座位。
成功條件：找出餐廳保留座位幾分鐘、免費取消的期限，以及一則評論給的建議；說出「我想訂兩人的位子」與「可以改成幾點嗎」兩句英文。
英文輸入：地圖搜尋結果 + 三則評論 + 訂位規則頁
Green Table · Thai · 4.5 ★ (312 reviews)
“Book early. It fills up after 7 p.m.” — Ana, 2 weeks ago
“Ask for an indoor table. Outside is noisy.” — Ken, 1 month ago
“The medium plates feed two people.” — Lia, 3 weeks ago
Booking rules
Free cancellation until 3 hours before.

Groups of 5 or more need a deposit.

單句模式

Tonight I want a good dinner near my hotel. I open the map app and search for Thai food. One place looks good, so I read the reviews. Green Table has four and a half stars. It has over three hundred reviews. One review says the place fills after seven. Another review says to ask for an indoor table. It says the outside tables are noisy. A third review says the medium plates feed two. These reviews help me plan before I go. I decide to book a table for tonight. I open the booking page and read the rules.

The page offers free cancellation until three hours before. So I can cancel this booking before four. It says they hold the table for fifteen minutes. I choose seven thirty for two people. The page says there is no space at seven thirty. Seven thirty is fully booked tonight. I call the restaurant instead. Green Table, good evening. How can I help? I would like to book a table for two. Sorry, seven thirty is fully booked tonight. Could I change it to six forty-five?

Yes, six forty-five is open. Could I ask for an indoor table, please? Indoor tables start at seven o'clock tonight. Could I change it to seven o'clock? Done. What name should I use? Mina. And when is the free cancellation? You can cancel it until four o'clock. I show up at six fifty, a little early. Hi, I booked a table for two at seven. Yes, Mina. Your indoor table is ready. The review was right about the noisy outside seats. I will read reviews before every dinner now.

🔗 Key Phrases
book a table Collocation今日目標
訂位子
打電話、線上訂位都用它；換成 book a room / book a ticket 一樣通。
free cancellation Collocation今日目標
免費取消
訂位、訂房、訂票頁的關鍵字；先找它，再看「幾小時前」。
Could I change it to ...? Fixed Expression今日目標
可以改成……嗎？
改時間、改時段、改班次都能直接套用。
fully booked Fixed Expression
訂滿了
網頁與店員都常用；聽到它就要準備問替代時段。
ask for Phrasal Verb
要求；指定
ask for an indoor table；指定座位、房型、尺寸都能用。
show up Phrasal Verb
出現；到場
訂位後準時到場就是 show up；沒到叫 no-show。

🎭 Role-play

你是旅客，先遮住答案再開口。第 5 回合與第 9 回合各有一次資訊改變，兩次都要用改時間的句型處理。

店員
Green Table, good evening. How can I help?
你
I would like to book a table for two tonight.
先說出今天的主要訂位句型。
店員
What time would you like?
你
Seven thirty, please.
店員
Sorry, seven thirty is fully booked tonight.
資訊改變一：想要的時段訂滿。
你
Could I change it to six forty-five?
馬上提出替代時段，不要停在原地。
店員
Yes. Would you like indoor or outside?
你
Could I ask for an indoor table, please?
依評論建議指定室內座位。
店員
Indoor tables start at seven o'clock. Is that okay?
資訊改變二：室內座位時段不同。
你
Could I change it to seven o'clock? And when is the free cancellation?
再改一次時間，並確認取消規則。

💡 Learning Tips
  • 今天最值得直接說的是 Could I change it to ...?；改訂位、改房型時段、改接送時間都能原句套用。
  • free cancellation 主要用來看懂訂位與訂房頁：先找到它，再看「幾小時前」與「保留幾分鐘」兩個數字。
  • 小提醒：book 不只是「書」，當動詞是「預訂」；看到 fully booked 就代表訂滿了。
