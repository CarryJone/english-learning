Finding the Walking Tour Meeting Point
播放速度
🎯 Mission
讀懂步行導覽確認信，找到正確集合點
你要從確認信與地圖找出開始時間、集合地點、提早抵達時間和辨認導遊的方法；到現場後開口確認，並處理下雨造成的地點變更。
成功條件：找出 9:30 開始、City Hall 外的噴水池、提早 15 分鐘、黃色雨傘；說出 “Is this the meeting point?”；下雨後改到 9:20 的博物館入口。
英文輸入：tour confirmation email + map result + rain update + guide conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
線上資訊判讀旅行開口公共英文日常反應
今天的能力證據：從確認信找出 9:30、fountain、15 minutes early 與 yellow umbrella；用 “Is this the meeting point?” 和 “How can I find the guide?” 現場確認，再依 rain update 改到博物館入口。

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I book a Saturday walking tour before visiting the old town. The confirmation email has a map and short instructions. It says, “Start time: nine thirty a.m.” “Meeting point: the fountain outside City Hall.” “Please arrive fifteen minutes early.” “Your guide will carry a yellow umbrella.” I search the address on my phone. The map shows two fountains near City Hall. I save a photo of the correct fountain. A blue arrow points toward the main square.

I reach the first fountain at nine ten. Several visitors are waiting there without a guide. I ask one visitor, “Is this the meeting point?” “I am not sure; my email shows another fountain.” I look up the tour title and address again. Both look correct, but the photo looks different. I walk to the second fountain beside City Hall. A staff member stands near the entrance. I ask, “How can I find the guide?” “Look for a yellow umbrella near the fountain.”
Suddenly, heavy rain starts across the square. My phone displays a new tour message. “Rain update: the meeting point has changed.” “Meet under the museum entrance at nine twenty.” I head toward the museum across the street. The guide now carries a yellow sign, not an umbrella. I ask, “Is this the meeting point?” “Yes, but we will start five minutes late.” “How can I find the guide after our break?” “Look for the same yellow sign near the bridge.” I thank her and join the walking tour. Now I can follow updates and find changed locations.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 先背起來主動說：Is this the meeting point? 與 How can I find the guide?。
  • 看確認信時先找 meeting point、start time、arrive early，不要逐字翻譯全文。
  • 看到 rain update 先找新地點與新時間，再依更新移動。
