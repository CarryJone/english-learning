Checking a City Bike Before a Museum Ride
播放速度
🎯 Mission
讀懂租借規則，安全借到城市單車
你要從租借 app 和站點公告確認價格、騎乘時間、歸還規則與營業時間；取車前檢查車況，若第一台車有問題，就開口要求替代單車。
成功條件：找出每日通票價格、每次騎乘時間與最後歸還時間；說出檢查車況、要求另一台車與請對方重複的句子。
英文輸入：租借 app + 站點公告 + 服務台對話
City Bike Rental
Daily pass: $8 · Each ride: 30 minutes
Return bikes to marked stations by 9 p.m.
A small deposit is required.

單句模式

I arrive at a city bike station before my museum visit. I open the rental app and read the rental notice. The app offers a daily pass for eight dollars. Each ride lasts for thirty minutes. The notice says I must wear a helmet. It says I should return bikes to marked stations. I write down the exact bike number first. The app shows twelve bikes available.

I choose a blue bike and check its tires. I check the bike for damage before I ride. The front brake feels loose when I press it. I walk to the help desk. Could you help me check the bike for damage, please? Let me check the bike number first. This bike needs repair today. Could I ask for another bike today? Please try the green bike near the library.

I read the rental notice again before scanning. The app asks me to pay a small deposit. I confirm the amount and start. I test the pedal and brake before leaving. I put on my helmet and follow the map. The bike feels steady, so I ride toward the museum. After the visit, I look for the return station. The first station is full, so I need another. Could you tell me where I can return this bike? Use the station near the library. Could you say that again more slowly, please? The library station is open until nine tonight. I read the rental notice for the closing time. It says the return station closes at nine tonight. I return the bike and wait for the app message. The app says the ride is complete. I check the final screen before my hotel.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 先讀租借 app，再記住 read the rental notice；它也能套用到租車、票券或景點規則。
  • 今天最值得直接說的是 Could I ask for another bike?；第一個商品、車輛或方案不能用時，可以換成 another option。
  • 取車前說 check the bike for damage，並留意 pedal、brake、helmet 的重音；單句播放時先看底線，再模仿節奏。
