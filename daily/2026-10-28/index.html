單句模式

I'm at my hotel, checking breakfast before tomorrow's trip. I've got my booking open and ask at reception. Hi, is breakfast included in my booking for tomorrow? Let me check your room number first. It's room twelve, and here's a screenshot of my booking. Yes, breakfast's included, so there's nothing extra to pay. Great, do I need a voucher for the dining room? Yeah, here's your breakfast voucher for tomorrow morning. Do I give this voucher to someone ? Right, just hand it to the person by the door.

Now I check breakfast against my travel plans. What time do you start serving breakfast? We open at seven, and breakfast ends at ten. I've got to leave at seven to avoid the traffic. No problem, we can put together a bag for you. Thanks, what can I get to go? We've got toast and fruit to take with you. Is there cereal with milk in the breakfast bag? Sorry, cereal's only for guests eating in the dining room. Okay, toast and a banana would be fine for me. We'll have it ready by reception at six forty-five, if you order tonight. Sorry, when do I need to order the breakfast bag? Tonight, and pick it up at six forty-five.

I check that the receptionist has my order. Can you put my name down for a breakfast bag? Of course, I'll put your room number on the list. Should I bring my voucher when I pick it up? Yes, please bring it to reception tomorrow. So I'll pick it up here, not in the dining room? Exactly, we'll have your bag right here for you. Thanks, I'll keep the voucher with my key. I've checked breakfast, placed my order, and confirmed the pickup.
