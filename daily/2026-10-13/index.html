單句模式

I'm outside my hotel, heading to the airport. I find the stop and ask the coach driver. Hi, does this coach go to the airport? Yeah, this one's going straight to the airport. Great, is the pickup point by this shelter? Usually, yeah, but we've moved it for today. Oh, I can see a sign on the shelter. That's right, it shows today's pickup point. It says every twenty minutes, so when's the next one? The next one's at ten twenty, and it's on time.

I check where to wait with the driver. Is the pickup point on this side of the road? It's by the hotel curb, just past the red door, not here. Sorry, which door did you say I should look for? The red door, on this side of the road. Okay, I don't need to cross the road, then. That's right, just walk back towards your hotel. Can I wait inside the shelter until the coach comes? Better wait by the red door so we see you. Got it, and how much is the ride today? It's twelve pounds, and one suitcase is included.

I check how to pay before heading over. The sign says card only, so you don't take cash? That's right, you can pay when you get on. Can I tap my card, or do I need a ticket? Just tap your card, and I'll give you a ticket. Thanks, can you help me put my suitcase in? Of course, I'll put it under the coach for you. So I'll wait by the red door for ten twenty. Exactly, keep your bag beside you while you wait. Thanks, my card's ready, so I'll head over. I know where to wait and how to pay.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 主動背起 Can I pay when I get on?；看告示先抓 pickup point 與 card only，別看到 shelter 就直接在那裡等。
  • 班距 every twenty minutes 不等於下一班立刻到。先問 when’s the next one?；沒聽清楚位置時用 Sorry, which door did you say?
