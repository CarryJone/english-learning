🎧 2-Minute Dual-Voice Practice
Mailing a Postcard from Abroad
播放速度
🎯 Mission
讀懂國際郵資標示並寄出一張明信片
你在旅途中買了明信片，要從郵局標示找出標準與大型明信片的郵資、寄送天數及購買郵票的位置；郵票機故障後，改到櫃檯確認尺寸與地址格式。
成功條件：找出 2 美元／3.50 美元郵資與 7–10 個工作天；說出 “How much postage do I need?” 與 “How long will it take?”；把 TAIWAN 寫在地址最後一行並投入藍色郵筒。
英文輸入：international postage sign + machine notice + post-office conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
公共英文旅行開口英文資訊判讀日常反應
今天的能力證據：從郵資標示找出 standard／large postcard 的價格、7–10 business days 與 Counter Four；看到 temporarily out of service 後改到櫃檯，詢問所需郵資、寄送時間與英文地址格式。

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I buy a souvenir postcard near the old town. I want to send it to my family in Taiwan. I look up the nearest post office on my map. It closes at six, so I walk there now. I fill in the address before I enter. A sign outside shows international postage. Standard postcards cost two dollars. Large postcards cost three dollars and fifty cents. Delivery takes seven to ten business days. Stamps are sold at the machine or Counter Four. I check my postcard against the size guide. I see a stamp machine near the entrance. The screen says “Temporarily out of service.” The machine is not working, so I join the queue.

“Hello. What would you like to send?” “This postcard is going to Taiwan.” “How much postage do I need?” “Let me check its size.” “It is slightly wider than the standard size.” “So it needs a large-postcard stamp.” “How much is that altogether?” “It is three dollars and fifty cents.” “How long will it take?” “Usually seven to ten business days.” “Great. Can I pay by card?” “Yes. Please tap your card here.”

The clerk gives me the correct stamp.

“Where should I put the stamp?” “Put it in the top-right corner.”

I place the stamp there and check the address.

“Wait. The country name must be in English.” “Thanks. Could you write it down for me?” “Of course. Write TAIWAN on the last line.”

I fix the address and show it again.

“Perfect. Drop it in the blue mailbox outside.”

I keep my receipt and leave for the hotel.

🔗 Key Phrases
fill in Phrasal Verb
填寫
文章用法：fill in the address → 在寄件前把地址填好。
write it down Phrasal Verb
把它寫下來
聽到重要拼字或地址格式時，請對方直接寫下來。
international postage Collocation · 今日目標
國際郵資
郵局網站或價目表常見標題；可用來找寄往國外的價格。
business days Collocation
工作天
7–10 business days 不一定包含週末與假日。
top-right corner Collocation
右上角
文章用法：把郵票貼在明信片右上角。
temporarily out of service Fixed Expression
暫停服務；暫時無法使用
在機器、電梯或售票設備上看到時，要改用其他方式。
How much postage do I need? Fixed Expression · 今日目標
我需要多少郵資？
不確定尺寸或寄送國家時，直接向郵局人員確認。
How long will it take? Fixed Expression · 今日目標
需要多久？
可轉移到寄件、維修、交通或等候時間。

💡 Learning Tips
  • 先從標示抓 international postage、價格、business days，就能判斷郵資與大約送達時間。
  • 最值得直接背的是 How much postage do I need?；想確認等待時間時直接問 How long will it take?
  • 看到 temporarily out of service 不必停住，改到 Counter Four；地址不確定時請對方 write it down。
