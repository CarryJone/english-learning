Fixing a Noisy Hotel Air Conditioner
播放速度
🎯 Mission
看懂飯店維修告示，並處理吵雜又不冷的冷氣
你要從房內服務卡找出安靜時段、維修櫃檯時間與緊急報修方式；打電話說明冷氣問題。今晚無法修好時，要詢問換房。
成功條件：找出安靜時段 10 p.m.–7 a.m.、維修櫃檯開到 10 p.m. 與緊急維修撥 0；說出 “The air conditioner isn’t working.”；最後換到安靜且冷氣正常的 314 房。
英文輸入：hotel room help card + maintenance notice + front-desk conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行開口日常反應英文資訊判讀公共英文
今天的能力證據：從告示找出 quiet hours、maintenance desk、10 p.m. 與 dial zero；用 “The air conditioner isn’t working.” 報修，今晚無法修好時用 “Can I change rooms?” 找替代方案。

單句：

I return to my hotel after a long afternoon. My room feels warm when I open the door. I turn on the air conditioner near the window. It starts, but the air is not cool. It also makes a loud noise every minute. I wait five minutes, but nothing changes. Then I read the “Hotel Room Help” card. It says “Quiet hours: ten p.m. to seven a.m.” The maintenance desk is open until ten p.m. For urgent repairs, guests should dial zero. A free desk fan is also available. It is nine fifteen, so I call now.

Good evening. How can I help you? The air conditioner in room five-oh-eight isn’t working. It blows warm air and makes a loud noise. Could someone check it, please? Yes, our maintenance worker can come in twenty minutes. Sorry, did you say twenty or forty minutes? Twenty minutes. Please keep the door unlocked. I understand, and I wait in my room.

The worker arrives with a small tool bag. I turn on the air conditioner again.

She checks the filter behind the front cover. The filter is stuck and needs a repair. I need another part for this machine. Can it be fixed tonight? Sorry, the new part arrives tomorrow morning. We can bring a fan for tonight. My room is still too warm for sleeping. Can I change rooms? We only have room three-fourteen near the elevator. Is that room quiet? Yes, and its air conditioner is working. Great. Can I get the new key now? Yes. Please pick it up at the front desk. I take my bag and move to the cooler room.
