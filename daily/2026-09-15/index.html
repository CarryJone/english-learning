Splitting a Dinner Bill with a Service Charge
播放速度
🎯 Mission
看懂帳單上的服務費，和朋友平分付款
晚餐吃完要結帳。你要從帳單和刷卡機畫面看出總金額、服務費有沒有含在裡面，再請店員平分帳單；朋友的卡刷不過時，改用你的卡幫他付。
成功條件：找出總金額 $44 與「10% 服務費已含」，刷卡機問小費時選對按鈕；說出「我們可以平分帳單嗎」，並在朋友的卡刷不過時說出「可以把他那一半刷我的卡嗎」。
英文輸入：餐廳帳單 + 刷卡機小費畫面 + 服務生對話
Luca's Pasta · Table 12
2 × Tomato pasta — $28.00
2 × Lemonade — $12.00
2 × Tap water — FREE
Subtotal — $40.00
Service charge (10%) — $4.00 · included
Total — $44.00
Card machine: Add a tip? [No tip] [10%] [15%]

單句模式

We're done eating at a little pasta place near our hotel. Excuse me, can we get the bill, please? Sure thing. How was everything? Really good, thanks. Glad to hear it. I'll bring it right over. Okay, what's the total? Forty-four dollars. That's with the service charge. Service charge? Is that like a tip? Yeah, ten percent. It says it's already included. Wait, did they charge us for the water? No, the tap water's free. Nice. So should we split it and leave something extra? Let's split it, but I'm not sure about a tip.

Our server comes back with the card machine. Hi, can we split the bill? Sure. Just half each? Yeah, half each works. No problem. That's twenty-two dollars each. I'll go first. Oh, it's asking for a tip. What should I tap here? Service is already on the bill, so you can just tap No tip. Sorry, so we don't add a tip? Right, no tip. It's already included. Good thing we asked. Great, that went through.

Then it's my friend's turn. Sorry, it didn't go through. Want to try again? Sure. It's a new card, so maybe that's it. Nope, still not going through. Oh no. And I don't have any cash. No worries. Can you put his half on my card? Of course. Just tap again when you're ready. Thanks. I'll pay you back tonight. No rush. Can we get a receipt, too? Sure, here you go. Have a good night. Next time, I'll look for the service charge first.

🔗 Key Phrases
split the bill Collocation今日目標
平分帳單；分開付帳
文章用法：Can we split the bill? → 我們可以分開付嗎？和朋友搭計程車也能換成 split the fare（平分車資）。
service charge Collocation今日目標
服務費
帳單、菜單、飯店客房服務都會看到；後面寫 included 表示已經算進總額，通常不用再另外給小費。
go through Phrasal Verb今日目標
（付款）成功、刷過
店員說 It didn't go through ＝刷卡沒成功；訂票網站寫 Your payment didn't go through 也是付款失敗。
get the bill Collocation
（請店家）拿帳單來
Can we get the bill, please? 美國餐廳常說 check：Can we get the check?
half each Fixed Expression
一人一半
店員問 Just half each? 回 Yeah, half each works.（一人一半就好）
put it on my card Fixed Expression
刷我的卡付
文章用法：Can you put his half on my card? → 他那一半刷我的卡。put 後面換成要付的部分就好。
pay (someone) back Phrasal Verb
還錢給某人
I'll pay you back tonight. → 我今晚還你。朋友先幫你付車票、門票時都用得到。

🎭 Role-play

你是旅客，先遮住答案再開口。第 4 回合先澄清服務費，第 5 回合把「服務費已含」和「選 No tip」藏在同一句裡，第 9 回合處理朋友的卡刷不過。

店員
How was everything tonight?
你
Really good, thanks. Can we get the bill, please?
先回應寒暄，再要帳單。
店員
Sure. Here you go. It's forty-four dollars.
你
Is the service charge included?
澄清：先確認服務費是不是已經算進總額。
店員
Yep, service is already on the bill, so you can just tap No tip.
資訊藏在句中：服務費已含，所以小費畫面選 No tip。
你
Sorry, so we don't add a tip?
只問你沒聽清楚的那一塊。
店員
Right, no tip. It's already included.
對方會用更短的句子重講一次。
你
Great. Can we split the bill? Half each.
說出付款方式：一人一半。
店員
Sure. Hmm, your friend's card didn't go through.
資訊改變：朋友的卡刷不過。
你
It didn't go through? Can you put his half on my card?
替代方案：先確認，再改刷你的卡。

💡 Learning Tips
  • 今天最值得背起來的是 Can we split the bill?；換成 split the fare 就能跟朋友平分計程車錢。美國餐廳常把帳單叫 check：Can we get the check?
  • 看帳單先找 service charge：寫著 included 就代表服務費已經算進總額，刷卡機再跳出 Add a tip? 時通常選 No tip 就好。店員講太快時，先問 Sorry, so we don't add a tip? 確認。
  • go through 主要練聽懂和看懂：店員說 It didn't go through、訂票網站寫 Your payment didn't go through，都是「付款沒成功」。遇到時換一張卡，說 Can you put it on my card?
