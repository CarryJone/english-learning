單句模式

I'm at the station, ready to travel to York. I get my ticket out and ask a worker. Hi, is this the ten forty train to York? Yes, it's the right platform, and it's on time. Great, I've got a reserved seat, but which carriage? Let me check your ticket for you. It says coach C, seat twenty-four, by the window. That's carriage C, and your seat's on the left. Thanks, does reserved mean I can use that seat? Yes, it's yours for this train, so keep your ticket.

Before the train comes, I ask about my bag. Can I put my big bag on the luggage rack? Sure, there's a large rack near the carriage door. Great, I won't need to lift it above my head. That's right, and keep the way to the door clear. Can I keep my small bag with me at my seat? Yes, under the seat in front of you. Okay, my ticket and phone will go there. You'll need your ticket later. That arrow points towards the front. That's the usual sign, but today's train is different.

The worker shows me where to wait instead. Carriage C's at the back today, not the front, so wait by me. Sorry, is my carriage at the front or the back? At the back, by this sign. At the back, got it, and do I get on here? Yes, wait here, then check the letter beside the door. I'll get on at carriage C and find seat twenty-four. Exactly, and let people get off before you get on. Thanks, I'll use the luggage rack and keep my ticket handy. You're welcome, have a good trip to York. I know where to wait and find my seat.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 車票上的 Coach C 在這裡是「C 車廂」，不是公路客運；carriage 也表示火車車廂。先背 I’ve got a reserved seat。
  • 方向聽不清楚時問 At the front or the back?，再確認 Do I get on here?；原本箭頭與現場說法不同時，先向工作人員澄清。
