🎯 Mission
看懂隨身行李液體規定，並處理超量瓶罐
你要從機場網站與安檢告示找出容量、透明袋和取出物品的規則；再向安檢人員確認一瓶防曬乳是否能帶過安檢。
成功條件：找出每瓶 100 ml 以下、全部裝入一個透明一公升袋、安檢時拿出袋子；說出 “Can I take this through security?”；瓶子超量時確認替代做法。
英文輸入：airport website rules + security sign + airport screening conversation

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I check the airport website before packing my carry-on. The page says, “Liquids must be in small containers.” Each container has a limit of one hundred milliliters. All liquids must fit inside one clear one-liter bag. The bag must close completely before security. At the airport, I read the security sign again. It says, “Remove the clear bag from your carry-on.” Large bottles cannot go through security. Empty bottles are allowed, so I empty mine.

I ask the officer, “Can I take this through security?” “Is it one hundred milliliters or less?” “Yes, the label says ninety milliliters.” “Then you can keep it in your clear bag.” “Do I need to take it out?” “Yes, place the bag in this tray.” I place my clear bag and phone in separate trays.
I follow the instructions and move toward the scanner. However, the officer notices my sunscreen bottle. “This bottle holds one hundred fifty milliliters.” I explain, “There is only a little sunscreen inside.” “The container size matters, not the amount inside.” I ask, “What should I do with this bottle?” “You can check it, mail it, or leave it.” My checked bag is already with the airline. I decide to leave the large bottle. “Can I buy sunscreen after security?” “Yes, there is a pharmacy near Gate Twelve.” “Does it open before my seven-thirty flight?” “It opens at six, beside the coffee shop.” I thank the officer and enter the security line. Now I know how to check liquid limits online.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 第一遍只抓 100 ml、one clear one-liter bag、remove the bag，先完成資訊定位。
  • 不確定能否攜帶時先問 Can I take this through security?，再確認容量。
  • 注意規則看的是 container size，不是瓶內剩餘的量；超量就問替代方案。
