英文輸入：票券使用說明（教學用模擬，只適用本篇虛構服務）
AIRPORT BUS: SAVE YOUR TICKET
1. Open your booking.
2. Choose Download ticket.
3. Open Saved tickets and check the barcode.
4. Turn off Wi-Fi and data, then open it again.
At boarding: show the whole barcode, not just the number.

I'm checking tomorrow's airport bus ticket. I open the help chat at my hotel before breakfast. Hi, can I save it to my phone? Of course, open your booking and choose Download ticket. Great, I can download it while I'm on Wi-Fi. Right, keep it in your saved tickets. Can I open it offline at the bus stop? Yes, this ticket works offline once you've saved it. Good, I won't need Wi-Fi outside. Yes, check it before you leave.

I follow the steps and find my saved ticket. I've found it, and I can see the barcode. Great, show that when you get on. Should I turn off Wi-Fi to check it works offline? Yes, turn off Wi-Fi and data, then open it again. Okay, it's still there, and I can show the barcode. Perfect, you've got your ticket ready. Can I turn Wi-Fi back on now? Sure, it's only off for that check. Thanks, I'll save it to my phone and keep it handy. Great, make sure your phone has enough charge.

I ask about a backup screenshot. A screenshot's fine here if the whole barcode shows, not just the number. Sorry, what needs to be visible in the screenshot? The whole barcode, not just the number. Got it, I'll take a screenshot with the whole barcode. That's fine for this bus ticket, and keep it clear. Can I show the barcode from my saved ticket instead? Yes, either one works here if it's readable. Thanks, I'll keep both ready so I can find them quickly. You're welcome, have a good trip to the airport. I've checked my saved ticket works offline before I leave.
