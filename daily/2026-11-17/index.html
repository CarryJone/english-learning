🎯 Mission
雨天換一把能打開的傘
你要去咖啡店陪姊姊過生日，紙袋裡裝著她的禮物。剛買的傘只打開一半，你回到車站商店請店員換一把。
✅ 從告示找出瑕疵商品能怎麼處理，以及要帶什麼。
✅ 出示收據，聽懂目前只能換大傘，再確認價格。
✅ 先試開替換的傘，再帶著乾燥的禮物去赴約。

單句模式

I'm meeting my sister for lunch on her birthday. Her gift's in a paper bag, and it's pouring outside. I buy an umbrella at the station shop before leaving. Here's your receipt, and there's a door to the street there. Thanks, I'll open it just outside the door. Under the roof outside, I press the button on the handle. The umbrella opens halfway and stops. I take it back to the counter.

Excuse me, I just bought this, and it won't open properly. Oh, let me try it under the roof. Yeah, the button's stuck. Can I swap it for another one? Sure, have you still got your receipt? Here you go, I paid by card a minute ago. Thanks, let me check what we've got left. We're out of the small ones, but you can take a bigger one today. Sorry, which size have you still got? Just the big ones. That's fine if it's the same price. It is, and you'll have more room under it too. Good, I need to keep this bag dry. I'll get one from the back for you.

He brings out a blue one and hands it to me. Can I try it under the roof before I go? Of course, I'll hold your bag while you try it. I step out with the new umbrella and press the button. There we go, this one opens all the way. Great, I'll take back the faulty one. Here's your bag, and keep the receipt for the new one. Thanks for sorting that out so quickly. I walk to the café with the bag under my umbrella. My sister pulls her gift out of the dry bag.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 商品出問題時，先說 It won’t open. 或 It won’t turn on.，再用 Can I swap it for another one? 提出更換。
  • 告示出現 faulty items 和 replacement 時，先看要帶什麼。這篇要帶商品和收據，換到能正常打開的傘後才出發。
