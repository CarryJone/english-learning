🎯 Mission
在機場購買並啟用行動數據方案
你剛抵達國外機場，需要比較兩個七日手機方案，向店員確認總價、設定服務與市區外的訊號，再解決啟用後仍無服務的問題。
成功條件：找出兩個方案的價格；確認是否包含免費設定；說出 “How much is it altogether?” 與 “Could you check it?”，並讓手機成功連上網路。
英文輸入：mobile plan sign + phone-shop conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行開口日常反應公共英文英文資訊判讀
今天的能力證據：從方案牌找出 10 GB／18 美元、unlimited／25 美元與 free setup；在手機店詢問總價、啟用 eSIM，並用 “Could you check it?” 處理沒有訊號的問題。

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I arrive at the airport terminal and connect to free Wi-Fi. It is slow, so I visit a phone shop. A sign shows two seven-day plans. The ten-gigabyte plan costs eighteen dollars. The unlimited plan costs twenty-five dollars. Both plans include free setup. I compare them and walk to the counter.

“Hi there. How can I help you?” “Hi. I need internet for one week.” “How much data do you usually use?” “Mostly maps, messages, and a few videos.” “The ten-gigabyte plan should be enough.” “How much is it altogether?” “Eighteen dollars, including the SIM card.” “Great. Can you activate it for me?” “Sure. May I see your phone and passport?” “Your phone uses an eSIM, not a physical card.” “Is the price the same?” “Yes. I can set it up now.” “Will it work outside the city?” “Yes, but the signal may be weaker in the mountains.” “Could you show me how to switch it on?” “Open Settings and select this new line.”

I follow the steps, but I still have no service.

“It is not working yet. Could you check it?” “Turn airplane mode on, then turn it off.”

The mobile signal appears, and I open my map.

“Perfect. Can I pay by card?” “Yes. Please tap your card here.” “What should I do if it stops working?” “Restart your phone or return to this counter.”

I save the shop location before I leave. Now I can find my hotel and send messages. My first travel task is complete.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 先從方案牌抓出 data、價格、free setup；這三項就能快速比較短期手機方案。
  • 最值得直接背的是 How much is it altogether?。手機沒有訊號時，不必解釋很長，直接說 It is not working yet. Could you check it?
  • 離開櫃台前，再問 What should I do if it stops working?，先記下故障時的下一步。
