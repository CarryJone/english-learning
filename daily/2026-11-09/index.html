單句模式

I'm at a bus stop with a sign about changes. I want to visit the museum, so I read the sign. Excuse me, does bus seven stop here today? No, this stop's closed today because of work on the road. Okay, where's the temporary stop for bus seven? It's outside the library, just down the street. Is that the library next to the park? Yes, it's only a short walk from here. Do I need to cross the street to get there? No, stay on this side and walk past the park.

I look down the street and see two café signs. Where exactly should I wait near the library? The stop's past the park, across from the café with the blue door. Sorry, which café is the stop across from? The café with the blue door. So it's across from that café, on this side? Right, stay on this side as you walk to the library. Thanks, I'll take a screenshot of the map before I go. Before I leave, I check which bus to look for.

Does bus seven go straight to the museum from there? Yes, you don't need to transfer on the way. Great, what should I look for on the bus? Check the display on the front for Seven Museum. If I see Eight Station, should I let it go? Yes, wait for bus seven with Museum on the display. Okay, I'll check the display before I get on. That's right, and wait by the yellow sign outside the library. Thanks, I know where to wait and what to look for. I walk past the park and find the yellow sign. The bus display says Seven Museum, so I get on.
