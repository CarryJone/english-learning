🎯 Mission
讀懂吊牌與退換告示，買到合身的外套
旅途第三天變冷，你要在戶外用品店買一件外套：先從吊牌與店內告示確認價格、試衣間規則與退換期限，再開口試穿；你的尺寸缺貨時，改問其他顏色並確認特價品能不能退。
成功條件：找出外套價格、退換期限與特價品規則；說出「可以試穿嗎」與「有沒有大一號的」兩句英文，並在缺貨時提出換顏色的替代方案。
英文輸入：外套吊牌 + 試衣間告示 + 退換規則告示
Blue Pine Outdoor
Light Jacket · Navy · Size M · $49
Fitting rooms: 3 items at a time
Returns within 14 days with the receipt.
Sale items are final — no returns.
Ask staff for other sizes and colours.

單句模式

The weather turns cold on my third travel day. I only packed thin shirts for this trip. I walk into a small outdoor store. A blue jacket near the door looks warm. I check the tag for the price and size. The tag says navy, size M, forty-nine dollars. A sign shows the fitting room rules. It says three items at a time. Another sign explains the return and sale rules. You can bring items back within fourteen days. The receipt must come back with the item. It also says sale items are final.

I take the jacket to the counter. Excuse me, can I try it on? Sure. The fitting room is behind you. I try the jacket on in front of the mirror. The shoulders feel tight when I move. It is a little tight on me. Would you like a larger size? Yes. Do you have it in a large? Let me check our stock for you. She looks at the shelf and the screen. Sorry, navy in large is out of stock.

Do you have it in another colour? Grey and green are in stock in large. Could I try the grey one instead? Of course. Here is the grey jacket. The grey one is not tight at all. Is navy out of stock in every store? Navy is out of stock here this week. So can I bring it back if it does not fit? Yes, within fourteen days with the receipt. Could you say the days again, please? Fourteen days. Sale items are final, but this is not. I pay and keep the receipt in my bag.

🔗 Key Phrases
try it on Phrasal Verb今日目標
試穿
受詞是代名詞時要放中間：try it on，不是 try on it。
out of stock Collocation今日目標
缺貨；沒有存貨
店員與購物網站都用它；反義是 in stock。聽到就準備換尺寸或顏色。
Do you have it in ...? Fixed Expression今日目標
有沒有……的？（尺寸、顏色）
Do you have it in a large / in blue / in a smaller size；買衣服、鞋子、紀念品都能用。
fitting room Collocation
試衣間
店內指示牌常見；有些店寫 changing room，意思一樣。
bring / take it back Phrasal Verb
拿回去退換
帶回店裡叫 bring it back / take it back；記得帶 receipt。
Sale items are final. Fixed Expression
特價品不退不換
不是「最後特價」；看到它就要當場決定，因為買了不能退。

💡 Learning Tips
  • 今天最值得直接背起來說的是 Can I try it on? 與 Do you have it in a large?；買衣服、鞋子、紀念品都能原句套用。
  • out of stock 主要用來聽懂店員與看懂購物網站：聽到它就代表要換尺寸、換顏色或換分店，不是「沒有這個商品」。
  • 小提醒：final sale 不是「最後特價」，而是「特價品不退不換」；看到它就要當場決定要不要買。
