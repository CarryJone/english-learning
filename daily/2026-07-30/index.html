Fixing a Hotel Key Card Problem
🎯 Mission
讀懂旅館房卡公告並請櫃台恢復進入權限
房卡突然打不開房門。先讀懂旅館公告，再向櫃台說明問題、出示身分證件，並確認卡片或房間的替代方案。
成功條件：找出錯誤嘗試可能封鎖房卡、櫃台要求的 ID 與免費重設資訊、服務到晚上 11 點，以及房卡仍失效時的新房間選項；最後說出修復請求。
英文輸入：hotel notice + front-desk dialogue
🗺️ Ability Focus
旅行開口公共英文日常反應英文資訊判讀
今天的能力證據：Mission: fix a hotel key-card problem and regain room access. Input: hotel notice and front-desk dialogue. Find the blocked/expired-card rule, ID + free reset, desk hours, and the new-room option. Say “My card does not work. Could you reset the card?” / “I can show my ID.”
📖 Today's Article
今日單字
複習單字
重要片語
🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

The door does not open, so Mina tries her key card again. A red light shows, and she checks the hotel notice. It says, “Too many wrong tries can block the card.” If your card is expired, bring it to the front desk. Please show your ID, and staff can reset the card for free.

The front desk is open until 11 p.m. The desk speaker says, “Please come down.” She says, “My card does not work. Could you reset the card?” The staff member checks the card and says, “It is blocked.”

Mina asks, “Do I need to show my ID?” She also asks, “What is another option if it still fails?” A new room is available, or we can deliver a card. Mina shows her ID, and the staff member can reset the card. Now she can access her room.

🎭 Role-play

你在旅館櫃台處理失效房卡。先遮住 You 的句子再開口；如果重設後仍失效，要詢問替代方案。

Staff
Hello. How can I help?
You
My card does not work. Could you reset the card?
Staff
I can check it. The card is expired. Please show your ID.
You
I can show my ID. Is the reset free?
Staff
Yes, it is free. Please wait one minute.
You
Okay. What is another option if it still fails?
Staff
We can give you a new room, or deliver a new card.
資訊改變：重設後房卡仍失效，改提供換房或送新卡的方案。
You
Thanks. I can access my room now.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 先從公告抓出房卡失效的原因、是否要出示 ID、重設是否免費，以及櫃台開到幾點。
  • 今天最值得直接說的是 My card does not work. Could you reset the card?；看到 show your ID，就知道要準備身分證件。
  • 如果重設後仍然不能用，先問 What is another option?，再選擇換房或請櫃台送新卡。
