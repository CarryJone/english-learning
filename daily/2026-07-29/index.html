Changing a Train Ticket After a Delay
🎯 Mission
讀懂火車 App 延誤公告並改搭較晚班次
你的火車延誤，可能趕不上下一班車。先讀 App 的改票規則，再向工作人員確認票券是否有效與新的月台。
成功條件：找出 6:10 直達車延誤 20 分鐘、票券仍有效且不需更改費、7:30 班次從 Platform 3 出發；最後說出確認句並處理聽不清楚的時間。
英文輸入：rail app notice + ticket-counter dialogue
🗺️ Ability Focus
公共英文日常反應旅行開口英文資訊判讀
今天的能力證據：Mission: read a rail-app delay notice and change the train plan. Input: delay notice and ticket-counter dialogue. Find the 20-minute delay, ticket validity, no change fee, and Platform 3. Say “Is this ticket still valid?” / “Could you say that again?” / “I will take a later train.”

重要片語
🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

The app message says, “The 6:10 express train is delayed twenty minutes.” Her next train leaves at 7:00, so she may miss it. Mina looks up the change rule in the app. The rule says, “You may take a later train today.”

It says, “Your ticket is flexible and stays valid today.” No change fee is needed. Mina asks a staff member, “Is this ticket still valid?” The staff member says, “Take the 7:30 train from Platform 3.”

Mina asks, “Could you say that again? The 7:30 train?” The staff member points to Platform 3 and repeats the time. Mina says, “I don’t want to miss my next train.” She checks the screen and sees the train is on time. She changes her plan and waits for the 7:30 train.

🔗 Key Phrases
look up the change rule Phrasal Verb

⭐ 今日資訊目標：在 App 或網站上查規則、時間或限制。

⭐ 今日資訊目標：看到 later train，就知道可以改搭較晚班次。
no change fee Collocation
不需要更改費
文章用法：no change fee is needed → 改票不用另外付費。
Is this ticket still valid? Fixed Expression
這張票還有效嗎？
⭐ 今日開口目標：確認票券、通行證或 QR code 是否還能使用。
Could you say that again? Fixed Expression
你可以再說一次嗎？
⭐ 今日修復目標：聽不清楚時間、月台或班次時，先用這句。
on time Fixed Expression
準時
文章用法：the train is on time → 看懂螢幕上的準時狀態。

🎭 Role-play

你在火車站櫃檯。先遮住 You 的句子再開口；中途月台從 3 號改成 4 號，請重新確認。

Staff
Hello. How can I help?
You
I need to look up the change rule. Is this ticket still valid?
Staff
Yes. Your ticket is flexible. Take the 7:30 train from Platform 3.
You
Could you say that again? The 7:30 train from Platform 3?
Staff
Sorry. Platform 3 is closed now. Use Platform 4 instead.
資訊改變：原本的 Platform 3 關閉，下一步改成 Platform 4。
You
Okay. Is this ticket still valid for Platform 4?
Staff
Yes. There is no change fee. The 7:30 train leaves from Platform 4.
You
Thanks. I will take a later train and not miss it.
