🎯 Mission
查看步道開放狀態，找一條能走到花園的路
雨後想走平坦的路去花園。先查公園網站，再向服務台確認從目前入口怎麼走到開放的步道。
✅ 找出哪條步道關閉、哪條開放。
✅ 找出花園步道要從哪個入口進去。
✅ 問清楚在哪裡轉彎，確認是否需要過橋。

單句模式

I'm at the main park entrance after a night of rain. I want a flat walk to the garden before lunch. I open the park website at the information desk. Hi, is this the page with today's trail status? Yes, we keep that page up to date. It says the riverside trail is closed, right? That's right, it's still muddy after last night's rain.

Is there another way to get to the garden? Yes, you can take the garden path from the south gate. Can I walk to the south gate from here? Sure, just follow the road outside the park fence. How will I know when I'm at the south gate? There's a little blue sign with the gate name on it. Is the garden path flat once I'm inside? Yes, it's flat, and you won't have to climb any steps.

I pull up the park map on my phone. Do I go across the bridge to get there? As you come up to the bridge, turn left just before you reach it. Sorry, did you say before or after the bridge? Before the bridge, turn left. So I don't go across the bridge at all? That's right, the road takes you straight to the gate. Will I see a sign for the garden once I'm inside? Yes, look for the green sign on your right. Thanks, I'll check the trail status again before tomorrow's walk. Good idea, we update it when a trail opens again. I head out and turn left before the bridge. At the blue sign, I go in through the gate. I follow the green sign and reach the garden.
