Storing Bags in a Station Locker Before a Day Trip
播放速度
🎯 Mission
讀懂置物櫃告示，順利寄放行李
你要在火車站讀懂置物櫃告示，確認大／中／小櫃價格、最晚取件時間與付款方式；大型櫃客滿時，改到人工寄物處寄放兩件行李。
成功條件：找出大型置物櫃一天的價格、最晚取件時間與付款限制；說出「詢問是否還有空位」與「改到哪裡寄放行李」兩句英文。
英文輸入：車站置物櫃告示 + 服務台對話
Station Lockers · Level B1
Small $4 · Medium $6 · Large $9 per day
Machines take exact change only.
Pick up your bags before 11 p.m.
Large lockers fill up fast on weekends.
Luggage room next to Exit 3 · $3 per bag
Luggage room hours: 7 a.m. – 10 p.m.

單句模式

I arrive at the train station with two heavy bags. My day trip starts at ten this morning. I do not want to carry them all day. I look for the station lockers first. A sign points down the stairs to Level B1. I read the locker sign slowly. A small locker costs four dollars a day. A medium locker costs six dollars. A large locker costs nine dollars. The sign says the machines take exact change only. It also says to pick up bags before eleven. Large lockers fill up fast on weekends.

Every large locker door has a red light. I walk to the station help desk. Excuse me, is there any space in the large lockers? Let me check the locker screen for you. All large lockers are full right now. Can I use a medium locker instead? A medium locker fits one small backpack. I have two bags, so I need more space. Then the luggage room is a better choice. Where can I drop off my bags? The luggage room is next to Exit 3.

Sorry, could you say that again more slowly? Go down to Exit 3 and turn left. What are the opening hours today? The luggage room opens at seven, closes at ten. One locker near the wall is out of order. I am glad I did not insert my coins there. At the luggage room, I drop off my two bags. Is there any space for one more small bag? Yes. Keep this ticket to pick up your bags. Do you take cards, or is it exact change only? Cards are fine here, but the lockers are not. I insert my card and pay six dollars.

🔗 Key Phrases
drop off Phrasal Verb今日目標
寄放；放下（人或物）
Where can I drop off my bags? 櫃檯、寄物處、飯店都能用。
pick up Phrasal Verb
取回；領取
pick up your bags；和 drop off 一組記，去程放、回程拿。
fill up Phrasal Verb
被占滿；裝滿
Large lockers fill up fast. 告示常用來提醒尖峰時段。
exact change Collocation今日目標
剛好的零錢（機器不找零）
看到 exact change only 就代表要自備零錢；售票機、公車都會出現。
Is there any space ...? Fixed Expression今日目標
還有空位／空間嗎？
問置物櫃、行李架、餐廳座位都能直接套用。
out of order Fixed Expression
故障中；不能使用
機器或電梯貼這張就別用；不是「順序錯了」。

💡 Learning Tips
  • 今天最值得直接背起來說的是 Is there any space in the large lockers?；換成行李架、餐廳座位、公車都能用。
  • exact change 主要用來看懂告示與機器說明：看到它就代表不找零，要先準備零錢再排隊。
  • 小提醒：drop off 是「放下、寄放」，pick up 是「取回、領取」，一組動詞片語一起記比較不會混。
