🎧 2-Minute A2 Dual-Voice Practice
Getting an Airport Tax Refund
播放速度
🎯 Mission
讀懂機場退稅步驟，完成退稅並處理櫃檯異動
你要從商店退稅指南與機場告示找出最低消費、海關位置、退稅櫃檯和營業時間；再向工作人員確認收據要求與現金替代方案。
成功條件：找出同店消費至少 $100、安檢前到 Customs Desk B、安檢後到 Level 3；說出 “Where can I get a tax refund?”；櫃檯改到 Gate 12 後仍完成選擇。
英文輸入：tax refund guide + airport notice + customs and refund-counter conversations
🗺️ Ability Focus
線上資訊判讀旅行開口公共英文日常反應
今天的能力證據：從指南找出 minimum purchase、Customs Desk B 與 Level Three；用 “Where can I get a tax refund?”、 “Do I need the original receipt?” 現場確認，再依 moved to Gate Twelve 的告示改變路線。

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I buy a jacket on my trip. The window says, “Tax-free shopping available.” I open the tax refund guide online. It says, “Minimum purchase: one hundred dollars at one store.” “Bring your passport and original receipt.” “Keep the price label on each item.” “Before security, visit Customs Desk B with the items.” “Then go to the refund counter on Level Three.” My purchase total is one hundred twenty dollars. The guide says I am eligible for a tax refund. I save the store address and airport instructions. I also check that my passport is not expired.

At the airport, I arrive early for my flight. Customs Desk B is near the departure hall. I show my jacket, passport, and original receipt. “Your purchase is eligible for a tax refund.” “Do I need the original receipt after customs?” “Yes, and please fill out this form.” “Where can I get a tax refund after security?” “Use Level Three, beside the pharmacy.” I write my hotel address on the form. The officer checks the label and stamps the form.
I pass through security and follow the blue refund signs. “A notice says, ‘Refund counter moved to Gate Twelve.’” “It is open until nine p.m. today.” I reach Gate Twelve, but it is busy. “A card refund takes about five business days.” “Sorry, could you say that again more slowly?” “The money returns to your card within five days.” “Where can I get a cash refund instead?” “Cash refunds are available at Counter Four downstairs.” “Do I need the original receipt there?” “Yes, and please show your stamped form again.” I choose the card refund and keep every document. Now I can read the rules and finish the process.

💡 Learning Tips
