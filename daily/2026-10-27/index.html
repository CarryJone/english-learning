✅ 從英文牌子找出「按表計費」與「接受刷卡」。
✅ 知道約 20 美元只是估計，車流會影響車資。

單句模式

I'm outside the station, ready to go to my hotel. I've got a suitcase, and I ask a driver. Hi, can you take me to the Park Hotel? Sure, I'll start the meter when we leave. About how much will the trip cost? About twenty dollars, but it depends on the traffic. So that's not a set price for the whole trip? Right, we use the meter to work out the price. Does this taxi use the meter even when traffic's slow? Yes, the price can go up in slow traffic.

Before getting in, I notice a card sign. Can I pay by card when we get there? Sure, you can pay by card here. Great, I don't have much cash today. No problem, just keep your card handy for the end. Can you put my suitcase in the trunk, please? Of course, I'll put it in the trunk. Thanks, and can you drop me off at the main entrance? Actually, there's a change at your hotel this morning. Oh, is there somewhere else you can drop me off? We can drop you by the side door, since the front's closed till noon. Sorry, which hotel entrance can you use today? The side entrance, not the front.

I check the new location before we leave. Is the side entrance far from the main one? No, it's just around the corner, next to a bench. Okay, please drop me off there, near the bench. Sure, I'll help you get your suitcase out there too. Thanks, can I have a receipt after I pay by card? Of course, I'll give you one when we get there. Great, I've got my card ready. I've checked the fare, payment, and where I'll get out.
