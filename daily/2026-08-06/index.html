Using a Timed Ticket at the Museum
播放速度
🎯 Mission
讀懂博物館預約規則並處理錯過入場時段
你要從官方網站確認週末入場與包包規則，帶著 2:30 的預約票到入口；若寄放包包後錯過時段，要向工作人員澄清並改到可用時段。
成功條件：找出週末必須預約時段、2:30 入場時間與大包寄放規則；說出 “Am I allowed to bring this bag inside?” 與 “Is there another time available?”，最後改到 3:30。
英文輸入：museum ticket page + entry rules + entrance conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
英文資訊判讀旅行開口公共英文日常反應
今天的能力證據：從官方票券頁找出 timed admission、2:30 time slot 與 small bags only；在入口確認包包是否可帶入，沒聽懂時請對方慢一點，再詢問另一個可用時段。

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

Tomorrow I plan to visit the City Art Museum. I look up ticket rules on its official website. The page says timed admission is required on weekends. Each visitor must book a time slot before arriving. I choose the 2:30 p.m. slot and pay online. Another rule says “Small bags only.” Large bags must go in a locker downstairs. Food and drinks are not allowed inside. I leave my drink at the hotel.

I arrive at 2:20 and join the entry line. A staff member smiles and scans my ticket.

“Welcome. Your admission time is 2:30.” “Thanks. Am I allowed to bring this bag inside?” “Let me check the size label beside the door.” “This bag does not fit the size guide.” “Small bags are allowed, but this one is too large.” “Please use a locker near the main hall.” “Is the locker included with my ticket?” “Yes, it is free for ticket holders.” “Great. Where can I find it?” “Go downstairs, turn left, and follow the blue signs.”

I put away my bag and return quickly. Now it is 2:34, and the entry gate is closed.

“Sorry, I missed my time slot.” “Can I still use this ticket today?” “Your ticket is still valid today.” “However, the next group is already full.” “I did not catch that.” “Could you say it more slowly?” “The 3:00 group is full.” “Is there another time available?” “Yes. You can enter at 3:30.” “Could you book that time for me?” “Of course. Please wait near the café until 3:25.”

That option works for me, so I keep my ticket ready.

🔗 Key Phrases
look up Phrasal Verb
查找
文章用法：look up ticket rules → 在官方網站查票券規則。
put away Phrasal Verb
收好；放到指定位置
文章用法：put away my bag → 把大包寄放後再回入口。
book a time slot Collocation · 今日目標
預約一個時段
票券頁常見說法；也可套用接駁車、活動或參觀預約。
timed admission Collocation
分時段入場
看到 timed admission，表示票券有指定入場時段。
join the entry line Collocation
加入入場隊伍
文章用法：到達後拿著預約票排入入口隊伍。
Am I allowed to bring this bag inside? Fixed Expression · 今日目標
我可以帶這個包包進去嗎？
在景點、場館或交通工具上確認物品規則。
Could you say it more slowly? Fixed Expression
可以說慢一點嗎？
聽不清楚規則或變更資訊時的 repair sentence。
Is there another time available? Fixed Expression · 今日目標
還有其他可用時段嗎？
原本時段額滿、錯過或不方便時，直接詢問替代時段。

💡 Learning Tips
  • 看票券頁先抓 timed admission、time slot、allowed，就能判斷入場時間與攜帶限制。
  • 最值得直接背的是 Am I allowed to bring this bag inside?；若原時段不能用，立刻問 Is there another time available?
  • 任務成功不是只看懂規則，而是確認新時段並把票留在手邊；聽不懂時先說 Could you say it more slowly?
