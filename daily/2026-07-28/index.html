Reporting a Damaged Suitcase
🎯 Mission
讀懂機場行李服務公告並完成送修流程
你的行李箱在機場受損。先讀行李服務櫃檯的公告，再向工作人員通報問題、保留標籤並把表格送到正確的櫃檯。
成功條件：找出要保留行李標籤、要拍照、表格要送到 Desk 5；最後說出要通報損壞行李並確認下一步。
英文輸入：機場行李服務公告 + 櫃檯短對話
🗺️ Ability Focus
英文資訊判讀旅行開口公共英文日常反應
今天的能力證據：Mission: report damaged baggage and complete the repair form. Input: airport baggage-service notice and counter dialogue. Find the photo, baggage-tag, and Desk 5 instructions. Say “I need to report damaged baggage.” / “Could you say that again?” / “Should I bring the form there?”

🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

At the airport, Mina goes to the baggage service counter. The sign says, “Report damaged baggage here.” It says, “Keep the baggage tag and take a photo.” Mina’s suitcase has a broken lock.

She says, “I need to report damaged baggage.” The agent asks, “Is this suitcase yours?” Mina says, “Yes. Can you replace the lock?” The agent asks Mina to fill out a form. Please write your name and flight number.

Mina asks, “Could you say that again?” The agent repeats, “Bring the form to Desk 5.” Mina asks, “Should I bring the suitcase there?” Yes. Take it to the repair desk. Mina keeps the baggage tag and completes the form.

🔗 Key Phrases
report damaged baggage Collocation
通報損壞行李
⭐ 今日開口目標：在機場或航空公司櫃檯說明行李受損；也可以說 report damaged luggage。
keep the baggage tag Collocation
保留行李標籤
⭐ 今日資訊目標：看到公告中的 keep，知道這是「保留」標籤，不要丟掉。
Could you say that again? Fixed Expression
你可以再說一次嗎？
⭐ 今日修復目標：聽不清楚櫃檯號碼、時間或下一步時使用。
fill out a form Phrasal Verb
填寫表格
文章用法：fill out a form → 填寫行李服務表格。
take a photo Collocation
拍照
文章用法：先拍下損壞處，方便工作人員確認。
bring the form to Desk 5 Collocation
把表格拿到 5 號櫃檯
文章用法：bring A to B → 把物品帶到指定地點。

🎭 Role-play

你在機場行李服務櫃檯。先遮住 You 的句子再開口；中途 Desk 5 改成 Desk 7，請處理資訊改變。

Staff
Hello. How can I help?
You
I need to report damaged baggage.
Staff

You

Staff

You
Could you say that again? Desk 5 or Desk 7?
Staff
Sorry. Desk 5 is closed now. Bring it to Desk 7.
資訊改變：原本的 Desk 5 關閉，下一步改成 Desk 7。
You
Okay. Should I bring the form to Desk 7?
Staff
Yes. We can replace the broken lock after you finish the form.
You
I will fill out the form and take a photo.

💡 Learning Tips
先從公告找出三個可驗收資訊：要保留 baggage tag、要 take a photo、表格要送到 Desk 5。今天最值得直接背的是 I need to report damaged baggage.；看到資訊或聽不清楚時，先用 Could you say that again?，再確認下一步。
