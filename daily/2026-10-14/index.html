單句模式

I'm at my hotel after several days away. My clothes need washing, so I ask at reception. Hi, can I use the laundry room tonight? Sure, it's downstairs, past the door to the garden. Great, how much is it to wash my clothes? It's four pounds, and the detergent's included in the price. I've got liquid detergent in a small container. That's fine, but you won't need your own. Does that include drying my clothes as well? No, the dryer's two pounds extra for each cycle.

I check the hotel's laundry page before paying. The page says last wash at eight, is that right? Yeah, start before eight so you've got time to finish. Okay, how long does the wash cycle usually take? About thirty minutes, then you can move to a dryer. Can I use this basket to carry my clothes downstairs? Of course, just bring it back when you're done. Thanks, and can I borrow an iron after the wash? Sure, we've got one here, just ask later. I'll pick it up later, after my clothes are dry. No problem, and keep the iron in your room overnight.

Before I leave, the receptionist mentions a change. The dryer's out of order, but the garden one's two pounds. Sorry, which dryer can I use for my clothes tonight? The one by the garden, two pounds a cycle. Got it, I'll use the dryer by the garden instead. That's right, take your basket with you too. Can I pay for both here, or do I pay downstairs? Pay here, and we'll give you coins for the machines. Okay, one wash and one dry cycle, please. That's six pounds, and here's your change. I've checked the cost and found a working dryer.
