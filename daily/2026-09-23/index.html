Getting Breakfast Before an Early Train

🎯 Mission
早班車前，讀懂旅館早餐資訊並領到外帶餐盒
明天要搭早班車。先讀旅館的早餐外帶頁，確認下單截止時間、領取地點與時段、兩種餐盒的價格和附餐；若原本想買的餐點售完，改選另一款，並向櫃檯確認房卡與最晚領取時間。
完成條件：找出今晚九點前下單、早上六點至七點十五分在大廳櫃檯領取；比較蛋三明治八美元（附小咖啡）與水果優格盒七美元（咖啡另加兩美元）；說出「Can I get breakfast to go?」並確認房卡和最晚領取時間。
英文輸入：旅館早餐外帶頁（情境資料）
Harbor Inn · Breakfast to Go
Order by 9:00 p.m.
Pick-up: 6:00–7:15 a.m. · Lobby Desk
Egg sandwich $8 · Small coffee included
Fruit and yogurt box $7 · Add coffee $2
Show your room key at pick-up
Breakfast card valid until 7:15 a.m.

單句模式

I'm at a small hotel by the train station. My train leaves early tomorrow, and I don't want to miss it. I need breakfast to go. I check the hotel's breakfast page on my phone. I can pick up a box at the lobby desk. Pick-up is from six to seven fifteen. I need to order before nine tonight. The egg sandwich is eight dollars and comes with coffee. The fruit and yogurt box costs seven dollars, plus two for coffee. The card is valid until seven fifteen.

I go down to the front desk. Hi there, how can I help you? Can I get breakfast to go tomorrow? Sure, which box would you like? I'd like the egg sandwich, please. Sorry, egg sandwiches are sold out tomorrow. Can I replace it with the fruit box? Of course, the fruit box is still available. Does a coffee come with the fruit box? The fruit box comes with yogurt, but coffee costs two dollars extra. Sorry, does it come with coffee? No, coffee's two dollars extra. Okay, I'll have the fruit box. Do I need my room key? Yes, show it when you pick up your box. I can pick it up at six forty, right? Yes, come before seven fifteen.

In the morning, I come down at six forty. Hi again, I'm here to pick up my breakfast. Your fruit box is ready now. Great, can I get a bag for the box? Sure, do you want a small coffee too? Yes, please, and I'll pay two dollars extra. No problem, your room key is all I need. Thanks, now I can catch my train. I take my breakfast and head to the train.

🎭 Role-play

你是旅客。先看店員回覆，再遮住英文，自己接話；餐點售完時改選其他早餐。

店員
Hi there, are you getting breakfast tomorrow?
先說出你要的早餐形式。
你
Yes. Can I get a breakfast box to go?
用 to go 說明要外帶。
店員
Sorry, the egg sandwich is sold out tomorrow.
資訊改變：蛋三明治賣完了。
你
Can I replace it with the fruit box?
提出替代品。
店員
Sure. It comes with yogurt, but coffee costs two dollars extra.
聽清楚附餐和加價資訊。
你
Does coffee come with the fruit box?
用 comes with 確認有沒有附咖啡。
店員
No. Coffee is two dollars extra. Show your room key at pick-up.
確認價格和房卡要求。
你
Got it. What time can I pick it up?

店員
From six to seven fifteen at the lobby desk.
