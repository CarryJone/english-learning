Using a City Bus Day Pass
播放速度
🎯 Mission
買一張市區公車一日票，並在正確站下車
你要從站牌找出公車號碼、班距、一日票價格與有效時間；上車後確認目的地。原本的市場站暫停使用時，要聽懂替代下車站並請司機提醒你。
成功條件：找出 Bus 8、每 15 分鐘、6 美元與午夜前有效；說出 “Does this bus go to Central Market?”；改在 City Library 下車並步行一個街區。
英文輸入：bus-stop sign + short driver conversation
🗺️ Ability Focus
公共英文旅行開口日常反應英文資訊判讀
今天的能力證據：從站牌找出 Bus 8、every fifteen minutes、$6 day pass 與 valid until midnight；用短問句確認目的地，沒聽懂時請對方再說一次，最後改在 City Library 下車。

重要片語
🔊 點擊播放單句
單句：

I need a bus to Central Market. I walk to the bus stop near my hotel. A sign shows Bus 8 and its schedule. Bus 8 comes every fifteen minutes. The sign also shows a day pass. The pass costs six dollars. It is valid until midnight. I can buy it from the driver. The bus arrives a few minutes later. I check the number before I get on.

“Hello. Does this bus go to Central Market?” “Yes, but the market stop is closed today.” “Please get off at the City Library.” “The market is one block away from there.” “Sorry. Could you say that again?” “Get off at the City Library.” “Then walk one block to the market.” “Thank you. I understand now.” “I would like a day pass, please.” “Sure. It is six dollars.” “Can I pay by card?” “Yes. Please tap your card here.”

I tap my card on the reader. The driver gives me a paper pass. I put the pass in my wallet. I sit near the front of the bus.

“Please tell me when we get to the library.” “Of course. It is about ten minutes away.”

I watch the street and listen carefully. Soon, the driver speaks to me.

“The City Library is the next stop.”

I thank the driver and press the button. The bus stops beside the library. I get off and check my map. I walk one block to Central Market. I keep my day pass for the next bus.

🔗 Key Phrases
get on Phrasal Verb
上車
文章用法：check the number before I get on → 上車前先看車號。
get off at Phrasal Verb
在⋯下車
文章用法：get off at the City Library → 在市立圖書館站下車。
day pass Collocation · 今日目標
一日票
站牌、售票機或交通網站常見說法；買一次可在當天繼續搭乘。
every fifteen minutes Collocation
每十五分鐘
用來看懂班距；不是只指某一個固定時間。
pay by card Collocation
用卡付款
文章用法：Can I pay by card? → 詢問是否可以刷卡。
Does this bus go to ...? Fixed Expression · 今日目標
這班公車會到⋯嗎？
上車前直接確認目的地，也可把 bus 換成 train 或 ferry。
Could you say that again? Fixed Expression
可以再說一次嗎？
聽不清楚站名時先用這句，不要假裝聽懂。
Please tell me when we get to ... Fixed Expression · 今日目標
到⋯時請告訴我
請司機或同行者提醒下車；換地點即可直接套用。
