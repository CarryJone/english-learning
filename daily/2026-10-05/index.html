🎯 Mission
旅館房間漏水：說明問題並確認換房
看懂旅館協助頁面的換房條件，告訴櫃檯漏水的位置；原本想要的雙人房尚未整理好時，確認可用的替代房間。
完成條件：找出換房是否加價、領新鑰匙的地點與所需物品；說明天花板漏水；接受單人房並確認房號。開口句：Can I move to another room tonight?
英文輸入：旅館協助頁面 + 櫃檯對話（模擬情境資料）
HOTEL HELP · ROOM PROBLEMS
Water in your room? Call reception.
Room changes are at no extra cost.
Pick up your new key at reception.
Bring your old key with you.

ROOM STATUS
Double room: not ready yet.
Single room 18: ready now, same floor.

單句模式

I'm in my hotel room after a day out. There's water near my bed, so I call reception. Hi, I'm in room twelve, and there's a leak. Oh, sorry about that, where's the water coming from? It's coming from the ceiling, right above the bed. Okay, is the bed wet as well? Yes, and the floor's wet near my bag too. I'm sorry, let's get you into a different room. Thanks, can I move to another room tonight? Sure, let me check what we've got ready.

I check the hotel's help page while I wait. It says room changes are at no extra cost. That's right, you won't have to pay anything extra. Great, I'd like a room with a double bed. The double's not ready, but there's a single at no extra cost. Sorry, which room can I move to now? A single room, at no extra cost. That's fine, I'm traveling alone, so a single works. Great, we've got room eighteen ready for you now. Can I pick up the new key at reception? Yes, just come down and bring your old key.

I pack my bag and head down to reception. Hi, I'm here to pick up my new key. Here you go, room eighteen's on the same floor. Thanks, can I leave my bag in the hall briefly? Sure, keep it beside you while we sort this out. I'll take my charger out of the old room too. Of course, check you've got everything before you leave. So it's another room at no extra cost, right? Exactly, and here's a clean towel for you too. Thanks for your help, I'll go up to room eighteen. I've explained the problem and got a room I can use.

💡 Learning Tips
  • 主動說 Can I move to another room? 和 Can I pick up the new key at reception?；讀協助頁面時抓住 at no extra cost，確認是否不加價。
  • 先說明水從哪裡來，再確認可用房型、費用與鑰匙領取方式；沒聽清楚就問 Sorry, which room can I move to now?
