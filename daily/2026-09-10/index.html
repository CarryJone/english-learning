Getting Back a Phone Left in a Taxi
播放速度
🎯 Mission
查懂失物回報流程，把手機拿回來
你把手機忘在計程車後座。先用朋友的手機讀叫車 app 的失物說明頁，找出回報步驟、司機回覆時間與歸還費用；再照步驟回報並跟司機通話，司機今晚無法送回時，改約失物招領處拿。
成功條件：找出司機多久內會回覆、歸還費用是多少、失物招領處在哪裡與幾點開門；說出「我把手機忘在你的車上」與「我什麼時候可以拿回來」兩句英文。
英文輸入：叫車 app 的 Lost item help 頁（四個步驟＋回覆時間＋費用＋失物招領處）
Ride App · Lost item help
1. Open Your trips and pick the ride.
2. Tap Report a lost item.
3. Fill in the item, the time, and your number.
4. We contact the driver for you.
Drivers reply within 24 hours.
Return fee: $15, paid in the app.
Lost and found: Station Road 5 · 9 a.m. – 6 p.m.

單句模式

I get out of the taxi at my hotel. Ten minutes later, I cannot find my phone. I think I left my phone in the taxi. I fly out tomorrow, so time is tight. I borrow my friend's phone and open the ride app. I find the lost item help page. Step one says to open Your trips. Step two says to report a lost item. Step three says to fill in the time. The app then contacts the driver for me. The page says drivers reply within twenty-four hours. The return fee is fifteen dollars.

I follow the steps and send the report. The app contacts the driver right away. Hello, this is your driver from Station Road. Hi. I left my phone in your car. I found a black phone on the back seat. That is mine. When can I get it back? I finish work at nine tonight. Could you bring it to my hotel tonight? Sorry, I cannot drive back to the city. Then where can I get it back tomorrow? I will leave it at the lost and found.

Which lost and found office do you mean? The one on Station Road, near the bus stop. Sorry, could you say the road name again? Station Road, number five. I check the office hours on the help page. It opens at nine and closes at six. Please reply in the app when you leave it. Sure. I will reply as soon as possible. I check the amount before I pay the fee. Fifteen dollars is fine for a lost phone. The next morning, the driver replies in the app. I get my phone back before my flight.

🎭 Role-play

你是旅客，先遮住答案再開口。第 5 回合出現「今晚送不回來」的資訊改變，第 10 回合練習聽不懂時的 repair。

司機
Hello, this is your driver from Station Road.
你
Hi. I left my phone in your car.
先把「東西忘在哪裡」講清楚，比 I lost it 更好幫忙。
司機
I found a black phone on the back seat.
你
That is mine. When can I get it back?
確認是自己的，馬上問取回時間。
司機
Sorry, I cannot drive back to the city tonight.
資訊改變：今晚無法送回飯店。
你
Then where can I get it back tomorrow?
改問地點，不要只說 okay。
司機
I will leave it at the lost and found.
替代方案：改放失物招領處。
你
Which lost and found office do you mean?
地點有多處時先問清楚是哪一個。
司機
Station Road, number five. It opens at nine.
你
Sorry, could you say the road name again? And please reply in the app.
聽不清楚先請對方重說，再要求後續回覆。

💡 Learning Tips
  • 今天最值得直接背起來說的是 I left my phone in your car.；把 phone 換成 bag、passport，把 car 換成 train、room 都能原句套用。
  • lost and found 是「失物招領處」，機場、車站、飯店的指示牌都會出現；有些地方寫 lost property，意思一樣。
  • 小提醒：lose 的過去式是 lost。I lost my phone. 只講「不見了」，I left my phone in your car. 講出「忘在哪裡」，對方才知道去哪裡找。
